The app creates the weekly schedule's sessions by itself. Add the other sessions yourself: an extra session or a pre-season session.

:::example
The “أقل من 17 سنة” (under 17) category goes running next Saturday, from 09:00 to 10:30. The goal: “جري 7.2 كم في 40 دقيقة” (run 7.2 km in 40 minutes).
:::

:::step Click the “Pre-season session” button
On a phone, first open the menu with the ☰ button. Open “Attendance”, then choose “Football” and “أقل من 17 سنة”. At the top of the page, click the white button with a flag. Its name, “Pre-season session”, shows when you hover the mouse over it. The “Pre-season session” window opens.
::figure att-calendar ring=att-add-preseason zoom=att-add-preseason
:::

:::step Check the type and the category
At the top of the window, “Pre-season” is coloured orange. In “Category”, the app chose “أقل من 17 سنة”, the calendar's category.
::figure att-add-preseason-modal ring=add-kind,add-category
:::

:::step Choose the date and time
In “Date”, choose the Saturday. The app writes 18:00 and 19:30: type 09:00 in “Start” and 10:30 in “End”.
::figure att-add-preseason-modal ring=add-date,add-times
:::

:::step Type the goal
Type “جري 7.2 كم في 40 دقيقة” in “Title / goal”. Is another category training with you? Tick it under “Other categories taking part”.
::figure att-add-preseason-modal ring=add-title,add-other-categories
:::

:::step Click “Save”
Leave “Player statuses on the list” as it is, and click “Save”. The message “Session added.” shows, and the session page opens. If “This category already has a session at that date and time.” shows, change the time.
::figure att-add-preseason-modal ring=add-save zoom=add-save
:::

:::step Record its attendance on the day
Record attendance like for any session, and click “Save”: the session becomes “Held”. Next to the month name, the orange badge counts the pre-season sessions held out of the season's target. It showed 1/12; now it shows 2/12.
::figure att-calendar ring=att-preseason-chip zoom=att-preseason-chip
:::

You don't see the two buttons? You need the “Add” permission for “Attendance”. Ask the club administrator for it.

:::tip
For one more training session, click the button next to it, with a + sign, named “Extra session”. The steps are the same, but it does not count toward pre-season. A quicker way: click an empty spot on a day in the calendar. The “Extra session” window opens on that day.
:::
