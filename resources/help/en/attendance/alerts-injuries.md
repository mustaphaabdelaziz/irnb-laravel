Two pages warn you: “Players at risk” and “Injuries”. You open them from “Attendance statistics”.

:::example
The “أقل من 17 سنة” category in football, this season. **عبد الرحمن بلعيد** missed 3 sessions in a row without an excuse: we print a letter to his parents. **رياض حمداني** has been injured for two sessions: we add the details of his injury.
:::

:::step Open “Attendance statistics”
On the “Attendance” page, click the button with four squares. Its name, “Statistics”, shows when you hover the mouse over it. The page opens on the branch chosen in “Attendance”.
::figure att-calendar ring=att-stats-link zoom=att-stats-link
:::

:::step Open “Players at risk”
At the top of the page, click the button with a warning triangle. Its name, “Players at risk”, shows when you hover the mouse over it. The page opens on “This season”. Under the fields are the two alert rules. The first: “Score %” below 60%, for players expected at 5 sessions or more. The second: 3 or more unexcused absences in a row. عبد الرحمن is in the table for both reasons.
::figure att-stats ring=stats-alerts-link zoom=stats-alerts-link
::figure att-alerts ring=alerts-rules
:::

:::step Click “Parent letter” on عبد الرحمن's row
The table is wide, and its end may be hidden on the right. Scroll it with the thin grey bar below it, or with your finger on a phone. At the end of عبد الرحمن's row, click the button with an envelope. Its name, “Parent letter”, shows when you hover the mouse over it. The letter opens in a new window. It gives his absences and late arrivals for the season. It lists every session he missed, was late for or left early. Its text comes from the “Parent letter” card in “Attendance settings”.
::figure att-alerts-letter ring=alerts-letter-first zoom=alerts-letter-first
:::

:::step Go back to “Attendance statistics”
At the top of the page, click the button with four squares. Its name, “Statistics”, shows when you hover the mouse over it.
::figure att-alerts-letter ring=alerts-stats-link zoom=alerts-stats-link
:::

:::step Open “Injuries”
At the top of “Attendance statistics”, click the button with a drop. Its name, “Injuries”, shows when you hover the mouse over it. Under “Currently injured” you see رياض, with the state “Ongoing”. An injury is a run of marks “Present, not training”, or “Absent (excused)” with the reason “Injury”. It ends at the player's first other mark.
::figure att-stats ring=stats-injuries-link zoom=stats-injuries-link
::figure att-injuries ring=injuries-current
:::

:::step Click رياض's name
Under “Currently injured”, click رياض's name. His page opens. The name is a link for those with the “View” permission for “Players”.
::figure att-injuries ring=injuries-current-name
:::

:::step Click “Add details”
The “Injuries” part is at the bottom of the “Attendance” card. Scroll down to it with the mouse wheel or the touchpad. On a phone, swipe the screen up. On the row of his injury, click the button with a pencil. Its name, “Add details”, shows when you hover the mouse over it. A window opens with “Body part”, “Details” and “Back on”. A return date up to today ends the injury. Only those with the “Edit” permission for “Attendance” see the button.
::figure att-injury-details ring=injury-add-details zoom=injury-add-details
:::

:::tip
The alert thresholds are set in “Alerts”, in “Attendance settings”. See “How do I change attendance codes, discipline rules and alerts”.
:::
