A session will not take place? Cancel it and write the reason. Attendance recorded by mistake? Erase it, and the session stays.

:::example
The pool is closed for maintenance on the day of the “أصاغر” session in “Swimming”: cancel the session. In “Football”, attendance for the pre-season session “جري 7.2 كم في 40 دقيقة” was recorded by mistake: erase its marks.
:::

:::step Open the session page
Open the “أصاغر” session from the “Attendance” calendar, as in “How do I record attendance for a session”. Under its title: the date and time, then the state “Planned”.
::figure att-session-actions ring=att-state
:::

:::step Click the “Cancel session” button
At the top of the page, click the red button with a circle and an ×. Its name, “Cancel session”, shows when you hover the mouse over it. The “Cancel session” window opens.
::figure att-session-actions ring=att-cancel zoom=att-cancel
:::

:::step Type the reason for cancelling, then confirm
In the “Reason for cancelling” field, type “المسبح مغلق للصيانة” (pool closed for maintenance). The reason is required: a session cannot be cancelled without it. Then click the red “Cancel session” button. The message “Session cancelled.” shows. At the top of the page: “Cancelled: المسبح مغلق للصيانة”. If attendance was recorded for the session, it stays saved but does not count.
::figure att-cancel-modal ring=att-cancel-reason
::figure att-cancel-modal ring=att-cancel-confirm zoom=att-cancel-confirm
::figure att-cancelled ring=att-cancelled-banner
:::

:::step Open the pre-season session
Attendance recorded by mistake? Do not cancel the session. You are now on “Swimming”: in “Attendance”, choose the branch “Football” and the category “أقل من 17 سنة”. Then open Saturday's session “جري 7.2 كم في 40 دقيقة”.
::figure att-calendar ring=att-branch,att-category
:::

:::step Click the “Delete session” button
At the top of the page, click the red button with a bin. Its name, “Delete session”, shows when you hover the mouse over it. The “Delete session” window opens.
::figure att-session-held ring=att-delete zoom=att-delete
:::

:::step Choose “Keep the session, erase its marks”
The window opens on “Delete the session”. Click the second choice: “Keep the session, erase its marks”. The red button becomes “Erase marks”. You don't see this choice? No attendance is recorded in the session, so there is nothing to erase.
::figure att-delete-modal ring=att-delete-choices,att-delete-reset
:::

:::step Click “Erase marks”
Above the button: “4 mark(s) will be erased for good.”. Click “Erase marks”. The message “Session marks erased.” shows. The session stays in the calendar, with the state “Planned”. Record its attendance again.
::figure att-delete-modal ring=att-delete-confirm zoom=att-delete-confirm
:::

These buttons need the “Edit” permission for “Attendance”. You don't see them? Ask the club administrator for it.

:::tip
Cancelled a session by mistake? Open it and click the bin button. Choose “Keep the session, erase its marks”, then click “Erase marks”. The session is “Planned” again, and its marks are erased if there were any.
:::

:::warning
“Delete the session” removes the session from the calendar and deletes its attendance from all statistics. It cannot be brought back. And the app does not recreate a deleted weekly-schedule session.
:::
