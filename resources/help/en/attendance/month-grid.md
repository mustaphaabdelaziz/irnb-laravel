Does the coach write attendance on a sheet at the pitch? Copy the sheet into the app, in “Month grid”.

:::example
The coach's sheet for the “أقل من 17 سنة” category. In one session's column, next to **أمين قاسمي**: “R15”, meaning 15 minutes late. Next to **عبد الرحمن بلعيد**: “AN”, meaning absent unexcused. The others' cells are empty: they are present. In the pictures, this column is already saved, as it looks after saving.
:::

:::step Open the “Attendance” page
On a phone, first open the menu with the ☰ button. In the menu, click “Attendance” under “Player Management”. You don't see it? Click “Player Management” to show it.
::figure att-calendar ring=nav-members,nav-attendance
:::

:::step Choose the branch and the category
At the top of the page, click “Football”. In the category field, choose “أقل من 17 سنة”. The month calendar shows this category's sessions.
::figure att-calendar ring=att-branch,att-category
:::

:::step Click the “Month grid” button
At the top of the page, click the button with three lines. Its name, “Month grid”, shows when you hover the mouse over it. The grid opens on the calendar's month: one player per row, one session per column.
::figure att-calendar ring=att-grid-link zoom=att-grid-link
:::

:::step Read the line of codes
Above the grid is a line of coloured codes. “P” present, and “B” present, not training. “AE” absent excused, and “AN” absent unexcused. After “R” and “D”, type the minutes: “R15” is 15 minutes late, “D10” left 10 minutes early. Your club's letters may differ: copy them from this line. Under the date of a saved session, a ✓ shows.
::figure att-grid ring=grid-legend,grid-help
:::

:::step Type أمين's code
At the top of the grid, find the session's column by its date. On أمين's row, type R15 in that column's cell. The cell takes a colour, and so does the column's date: it changed but is not saved yet. Leave a present player's cell empty. On a phone, swipe the grid to the left with your finger to see the other columns.
::figure att-grid-typed ring=grid-cell-first zoom=grid-cell-first
:::

:::step Move to عبد الرحمن's cell
The arrow keys on the keyboard move you between cells, each in its own direction. The Enter key goes down to the next row. عبد الرحمن is on the first row: press the ↑ arrow twice, then type AN. On a phone, tap the cell with your finger.
::figure att-grid-moved ring=grid-cell-first zoom=grid-cell-first
:::

:::step Click “Save”
At the top of the page, click “Save”. The app saves only the columns you changed. Their empty cells are saved as “Present”. The message “Attendance saved.” shows, and a ✓ shows under the session's date. Saving needs the “Edit” permission for “Attendance”.
::figure att-grid-moved ring=grid-save zoom=grid-save
:::

An unknown code? Its cell gets a red border, and no column is saved. Fix it, then click “Save” again.

“AE” is saved with the reason “Other”, unless the player already had a saved reason. To choose the reason, click the session's date at the top of the grid: its page opens.

The cell “—” means the player is not on that session's list. A cancelled session does not show in the grid. For another month, change the month in the calendar, then open the grid.

You can't type in the cells? You need the “Edit” permission for “Attendance”. Ask the club administrator for it.

:::tip
Print the blank sheet for the coach before the month starts. At the top of the grid, click the printer button. Its name, “Print sheet”, shows when you hover the mouse over it. It has the same players, sessions and codes. The button next to it, “Print with marks”, prints it with the saved codes.
:::
