“Attendance statistics” gathers the figures of held sessions. From there you open “Attendance ranking” for one category, and print the top players' certificates.

:::example
The “أقل من 17 سنة” category in football, this season. The club rewards its most regular players. First is **أمين قاسمي**, then **رياض حمداني** and **أنس لعريبي**. We print the three certificates.
:::

:::step Open “Attendance statistics”
On the “Attendance” page, click the button with four squares. Its name, “Statistics”, shows when you hover the mouse over it. The page opens on the branch chosen in “Attendance”, and on “This month”. Marks and points come only from held sessions. A “Score %” of 100% means everyone attended every session. Late arrivals and absences lower it.
::figure att-calendar ring=att-stats-link zoom=att-stats-link
::figure att-stats ring=stats-tiles
:::

:::step Open “Attendance ranking”
At the top of the page, click the button with a flag. Its name, “Attendance ranking”, shows when you hover the mouse over it. The page opens on the branch's first category, and on “Month”.
::figure att-stats ring=stats-ranking-link zoom=stats-ranking-link
:::

:::step Choose “أقل من 17 سنة”
In the category field, choose “أقل من 17 سنة”. This month's ranking shows. Only players expected at 5 sessions or more are ranked. So early in the month, nobody may show.
::figure att-ranking-month ring=ranking-category
:::

:::step Choose “Season”
In the field next to the category, choose “Season”. The current season's ranking shows: أمين is first with 96.4%. The ranking follows “Score %”. On a tie, the player with fewer unexcused absences comes first, then the one with fewer late arrivals.
::figure att-ranking-month ring=ranking-type
::figure att-ranking
:::

:::step Click “Print top 3 certificates”
At the top of the page, click the button with a printer. Its name, “Print top 3 certificates”, shows when you hover the mouse over it. The certificates open in a new window, one certificate per page. At the end of each row, a small printer button prints one player's certificate. Its name, “Certificate”, shows when you hover the mouse over it.
::figure att-ranking ring=ranking-print-top3,ranking-certificate-first zoom=ranking-certificate-first
:::

:::tip
Each status's points and the discipline rules are set in “Attendance settings”. See “How do I change attendance codes, discipline rules and alerts”.
:::
