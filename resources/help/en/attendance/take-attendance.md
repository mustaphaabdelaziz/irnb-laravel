Record attendance on the session page: one player per row, with the status buttons next to them.

:::example
At the next “أقل من 17 سنة” (under 17) session, **أمين قاسمي** arrives 10 minutes late. **عبد الرحمن بلعيد** is absent because he is ill. And **وليد سعداوي**, from “أكابر” (seniors), trains with them.
:::

:::step Open the “Attendance” page
On a phone, first open the menu with the ☰ button. In the menu, click “Attendance” under “Player Management”. You don't see it? Click “Player Management” to show it.
::figure att-calendar ring=nav-members,nav-attendance
:::

:::step Choose the branch and the category
At the top of the page, click “Football”. In the category field, choose “أقل من 17 سنة”. The month calendar shows this category's sessions.
::figure att-calendar ring=att-branch,att-category
:::

:::step Open the session
In the calendar, click the session on its day: a grey box marked 18:00. Held sessions are green, with a ✓. The session page opens, and at the top: “Not recorded yet: everyone is shown as present until you save.”.
::figure att-calendar ring=att-session-next zoom=att-session-next
::figure att-session ring=att-not-saved,att-roster
:::

:::step Mark أمين as late
On أمين's row, click “Late”. The “Minutes” field shows below it: type 10. “Late” is not saved without minutes.
::figure att-session-late ring=att-row-late,att-minutes zoom=att-minutes
:::

:::step Mark عبد الرحمن as absent with an excuse
On عبد الرحمن's row, click “Absent (excused)”. The “Reason” field shows, set to “Other”. Choose “Illness” in it.
::figure att-session-excused ring=att-row-excused,att-reason zoom=att-reason
:::

:::step Add وليد to the session
Under the last player, type “وليد” in the field “Search a player by name to add…”. Click his name in the list that shows. He is added at the end of the session, with the status “Present”.
::figure att-session-guest ring=att-guest,att-guest-result
:::

:::step Click “Save”
In the “Coach” field, the app wrote the previous session's coach. Correct it if it changed. Click “Save”. The message “Attendance saved.” shows, and the session's state becomes “Held”. Saving needs the “Edit” permission for “Attendance”.
::figure att-session-save ring=att-coach,att-save zoom=att-save
:::

You can search for any player in the club. If they are from another branch, their branches show next to their name.

:::tip
Everyone came? Click “Save” anyway. Before saving, everyone shows as “Present”, but nothing is recorded. The session stays “Planned” and does not count in the statistics.
:::
