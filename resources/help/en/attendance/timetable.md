Enter each category's training days and hours in “Attendance settings”. The app puts the sessions in the calendar, week after week.

:::example
The “أقل من 17 سنة” (under 17) category in “Football” trains on Monday and Wednesday, from 18:00 to 19:30. The coach adds a session on **Friday**, at the same hours. Then the club closes for three days for “عطلة مدرسية” (school holidays).
:::

:::step Open the “Attendance” page
On a phone, first open the menu with the ☰ button. In the menu, click “Attendance” under “Player Management”. You don't see it? Click “Player Management” to show it.
::figure att-calendar ring=nav-members,nav-attendance
:::

:::step Click the “Attendance settings” button
At the top of the page, click the button with three lines, each with a small circle on it. Its name, “Attendance settings”, shows when you hover the mouse over it. The “Attendance settings” page opens. You don't see the button? You need the “Edit” permission for “Attendance”.
::figure att-calendar ring=att-settings zoom=att-settings
:::

:::step Fill in the Friday session row
In the “Weekly schedule” card, choose “Football” in “Branch”. In “Category”, choose “أقل من 17 سنة”. In “Day”, choose “Friday”. “Start” 18:00 and “End” 19:30 are already filled in. “From” holds today's date: the schedule starts on that day. Leave “Until” empty: the schedule goes on with no end.
::figure set-schedule ring=set-schedule-form
:::

:::step Click “Add” under the fields
In the “Weekly schedule” card, click “Add”. The message “Schedule saved.” shows. The Friday row appears in the list, with the Monday and Wednesday rows.
::figure set-schedule ring=set-schedule-add zoom=set-schedule-add
:::

:::step Enter the closure days
In the “Club closures” card, choose the first day in “Start date”. Choose the last day in “End date”. In “Reason”, type “عطلة مدرسية”. The reason is required.
::figure set-closure-filled ring=set-closure-form
:::

:::step Click “Add” in the closures card
In the “Club closures” card, click “Add”. The message “Closure saved.” shows. The period appears under the fields. The app deletes the weekly schedule's planned sessions on these days, and puts no sessions on them.
::figure set-closure-filled ring=set-closure-add zoom=set-closure-add
::figure set-closure ring=set-closure-list
:::

A closure does not delete a session whose attendance is recorded, nor a session you added by hand.

At the end of each “Weekly schedule” row, a pencil button changes the row, and a bin button deletes it. Either way, its upcoming sessions with no recorded attendance are deleted. After a change, the app puts in the new row's sessions.

Does a category train in more than one group? In the “Groups” card, on the same page, add a group for each one. Then press the “Group players” button on its line and tick its players. The “Group” choice then appears in the “Weekly schedule” card: choose the group of each line. A group's session lists only its players. A player who is in no group does not appear in group sessions; the card reminds you how many players are without a group.

Deleting a closure with the bin button puts those days' sessions back in the calendar.

The “Pre-season targets” card keeps the number of planned pre-season sessions for each category, from 0 to 200, for the current or next season. In the calendar, the “Pre-season” box shows the progress: “1/12” means one session held out of 12.

:::tip
Don't add the week's sessions one by one. After “Add”, they appear in the calendar by themselves every week, from the “From” date.
:::
