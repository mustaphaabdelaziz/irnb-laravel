Every board member is a player registered in the club. The app takes their name, phone and photo from the player's file. If the file changes, these change too.

:::example
هشام بوعلام joins the current board with the role “Member”.
:::

:::step Open the “Board Members” page
On a phone, first open the menu with the ☰ button. In the menu, click “Board Members” under “Board of Directors”. The organigram of the current term appears. At the top is “President”, then the bureau row, then the committees.
::figure board-add-member-page ring=nav-board-members
:::

:::step An empty seat? Click its box
An empty box has a dashed border and a + sign. For example “Head” in “لجنة الإعلام” (media committee). Click it, and the list of players appears. Careful: clicking a name saves it at once, with no “Save”. You need the “Add” permission on “Board”. In our example, هشام has no seat and no committee, so follow the next step.
::figure board-add-member-empty ring=board-seat-3-head-1
:::

:::step Click the “Add Member” button
Above the organigram, click the button with the + sign. Hover over it to see its name: “Add Member”. A window with the same name opens. Can't see the button? You need the “Add” permission on “Board”.
::figure board-add-member-page ring=board-add-member zoom=board-add-member
:::

:::step Search for the player and pick them
In “Player”, type: بوعلام. The matching players appear, except archived ones. Click: بوعلام هشام بن جمال بن مصطفى. Their name fills “Player”. Nothing is saved yet.
::figure board-add-member-player ring=board-form-player,board-form-player-10
:::

:::step Check “Role”, “Status” and “Term”
The window opens on “Member”, “Active” and the term the page shows. That is what the example wants, so leave them. For another role, choose it in “Role”. “Former” in “Status” puts the member under “Former members”.
::figure board-add-member-form ring=board-form-role,board-form-status,board-form-term
:::

:::step Click “Save”
The message “Board member added.” appears. هشام shows under “Other members”, because he has no committee. Was the player already in this term? They are not added twice. Their role and status change, and “Board member updated.” appears.
::figure board-add-member-form ring=board-form-save zoom=board-form-save
:::

:::step Click a member's box to see their card
Click a box with a name, such as “President”. The member's card appears: name, role, phone, and committees if any. Click the WhatsApp icon next to the phone to open a chat with them.
::figure board-add-member-page ring=board-box-president
::figure board-add-member-seat ring=board-seat-whatsapp zoom=board-seat-whatsapp
:::
