A meeting won't take place? Cancel it and write the reason. It stays in the history and no longer changes. Deleting is for a meeting entered by mistake.

:::example
The general assembly is postponed with no new date: cancel “اجتماع تحضير الجمعية العامة” (general assembly preparation). If the same meeting was entered twice, delete the extra copy.
:::

:::step Open the meeting from the “Meetings” page
On a phone, first open the menu with the ☰ button. In the menu, click “Meetings” under “Board of Directors”. Then click: اجتماع تحضير الجمعية العامة.
::figure board-schedule-meeting-list ring=nav-board-meetings,meet-row-3
:::

:::step Click the “Cancel meeting” button
At the top of the page, click the button with a × in a circle. Hover over it to see its name: “Cancel meeting”. The button only shows on a “Scheduled” meeting. A meeting that was held can't be cancelled. The button needs the “Edit” permission on “Board”.
::figure board-cancel-delete-meeting-page ring=meet-cancel zoom=meet-cancel
:::

:::step Write the “Reason for cancellation”, then click “Cancel meeting”
The “Cancel meeting” window opens. In “Reason for cancellation”, type: تأجيل الجمعية العامة إلى موعد لاحق. The reason is required, at least three characters. Click “Cancel meeting” in the window. The message “Meeting cancelled.” appears. Changed your mind? Click “Keep meeting”, and nothing changes.
::figure board-cancel-delete-meeting-cancel ring=meet-cancel-reason,confirm-ok
:::

:::step Open a cancelled meeting to see how it looks
In “Meetings”, click: لقاء مع الممولين. In the list its title is struck through, with “Cancelled” next to it. At the top of its page: “Cancelled”, the cancellation date and who cancelled it. Then the “Reason for cancellation”, and “A cancelled meeting can no longer be edited.”. In “Status” you read “Cancelled”. Its fields and attendance no longer change. No attendance was saved for it, so no member has a mark. It stays in the list, also under the “Cancelled” button.
::figure board-schedule-meeting-list ring=meet-row-4
::figure board-cancel-delete-meeting-cancelled ring=meet-cancelled-banner,meet-status,meet-attendance
:::

:::step To delete a meeting, click the “Delete meeting” button
Open the extra copy. At the top of the page, click the trash button. Hover over it to see its name: “Delete meeting”. The meeting is deleted whatever its status, even a cancelled one. In “Meetings”, the same trash button is on each meeting's row. The button needs the “Delete” permission on “Board”.
::figure board-cancel-delete-meeting-page ring=meet-delete zoom=meet-delete
:::

:::step Confirm the deletion
The “Delete meeting” window asks you to confirm. The meeting, its attendance and its minutes file are erased for good. Its tasks are kept. Click “Delete meeting” in the window. The message “Meeting deleted.” appears, and you go back to “Meetings”. The tasks stay on the “Tasks” page, with no meeting.
::figure board-cancel-delete-meeting-delete ring=confirm-ok
:::

:::warning
⚠️ Deleting can't be undone. Cancelling can't either: “The meeting stays in the history, marked as cancelled. This cannot be undone.”. Only the date changed, and you know the new one? Fix the “Date”, then click “Save Minutes”.
:::
