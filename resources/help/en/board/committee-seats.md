Each committee has a card in the organigram. It holds one “Head”, two deputies, and any number of members. You fill the boxes by clicking them directly.

:::example
هشام بوعلام becomes “Head” of “لجنة الإعلام” (media committee). كمال درارجة moves from “Member” to “Committee deputy” in “اللجنة التقنية” (technical committee).
:::

:::step Open the “Board Members” page
On a phone, first open the menu with the ☰ button. In the menu, click “Board Members” under “Board of Directors”. Below the bureau row are the committee cards. Each card shows “Head”, then two “Committee deputy” boxes, then “Members”. An empty box has a dashed border and a + sign.
::figure board-add-member-empty ring=nav-board-members
:::

:::step Click the empty “Head” box
In the “لجنة الإعلام” card, click “Head”. A window opens with the title “لجنة الإعلام — Head”. It lists the players, except archived ones. For a plain member, click “Add member” under “Members”. You need the “Add” permission on “Board”.
::figure board-add-member-empty ring=board-seat-3-head-1
:::

:::step Search for the player and click their name
In the search box, type: بوعلام. Click: بوعلام هشام بن جمال بن مصطفى. Careful: the click saves at once, with no “Save”. The message “Committee seat saved.” appears. هشام was not on the board, so he joins the term with the role “Member”. An active member of this term shows “On the board” next to their name. They keep their role and take the committee seat. If you pick a “Former” member of this term, they take the seat and become “Active” again.
::figure board-committee-seats-picker ring=board-picker-10
:::

:::step To change a seat, click the member's box
In “اللجنة التقنية”, click the box of كمال درارجة under “Members”. His card appears. It shows the committee name and three buttons: “Head”, “Committee deputy” and “Member”. The coloured button is his current seat.
::figure board-add-member-empty ring=board-seat-2-member-1
:::

:::step Click “Committee deputy”
The seat is saved at once, and “Committee seat saved.” appears. كمال moves to a “Committee deputy” box. If that seat is full, you see “This seat is full: one head and two deputies per committee.”. First move the current holder to “Member”, or take them off the committee. The buttons need the “Edit” permission on “Board”.
::figure board-committee-seats-menu ring=board-seat-pos-deputy zoom=board-seat-pos-deputy
:::

:::step To take a member off the committee, click “Remove from committee” and confirm
For example, كمال leaves the committee later. On his card, click the button with the arrow going out. Hover over it to see its name: “Remove from committee”. A window asks: “درارجة كمال will leave “اللجنة التقنية” and stays on the board.” Click “Remove from committee”. The message “Removed from the committee.” appears. كمال stays on the board with his role, under “Other members”. The button needs the “Delete” permission on “Board”.
::figure board-committee-seats-menu ring=board-seat-leave zoom=board-seat-leave
::figure board-committee-seats-leave-confirm ring=confirm-ok
:::

:::tip
A bureau member can also sit on a committee. نبيل عثماني is “Treasurer”, and he is “Head” of “اللجنة المالية” (finance committee). One member can sit on several committees. Their committee seat does not change their bureau role.
:::
