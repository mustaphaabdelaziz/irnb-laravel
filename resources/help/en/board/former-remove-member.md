---
title: How do I make a member former or remove them from the board
summary: Someone who leaves the board becomes “Former” and keeps their history. Someone added by mistake is removed from the board.

A board member left before the end of the term? Make them “Former”. They leave the organigram and keep their history. “Remove from board” is for someone added by mistake.

:::example
ليلى بوعزة resigns in the middle of the term, so she becomes “Former”. If رشيد مقران was added by mistake, he is removed from the board.
:::

:::step Open the “Board Members” page
On a phone, first open the menu with the ☰ button. In the menu, click “Board Members” under “Board of Directors”. The organigram of the current term appears.
::figure board-add-member-empty ring=nav-board-members
:::

:::step Click the member's box
ليلى has no committee, so her box is under “Other members”. Click it, and her card appears.
::figure board-add-member-empty ring=board-other-13
:::

:::step Click the pencil button
On the card, click the pencil button. Hover over it to see its name: “Edit”. A window with the same name opens. The button needs the “Edit” permission on “Board”.
::figure board-former-remove-member-menu ring=board-seat-edit zoom=board-seat-edit
:::

:::step In “Status”, choose “Former”
Leave “Role” and “Term” as they are. Was the member on a committee? They leave it on their own when you save. Their seat becomes free, so you can give it to another member.
::figure board-former-remove-member-form ring=board-form-status
:::

:::step Click “Save”
The message “Board member updated.” appears. ليلى leaves the organigram. You find her in “Former members” below the organigram, with مراد زيتوني. Her past attendance and tasks stay under her name. But she no longer shows in the attendance of new meetings, and she is not copied to a new term. To bring her back, choose “Active” the same way. Committee seats don't come back on their own.
::figure board-former-remove-member-form ring=board-form-save zoom=board-form-save
::figure board-former-remove-member-list ring=board-former-toggle
:::

:::step For someone added by mistake, click their box
رشيد مقران is “Committee deputy” in “اللجنة المالية” (finance committee). Click his box, and his card appears.
::figure board-add-member-empty ring=board-seat-1-deputy-1
:::

:::step Click “Remove from board” and confirm
On the card, click the trash button. Hover over it to see its name: “Remove from board”. The “Remove from board” window asks: “مقران رشيد will be removed from the board for good. Past meeting attendance stays under their name; their tasks become “Unassigned”.” Click “Delete”. The message “Board member removed.” appears. The button needs the “Delete” permission on “Board”.
::figure board-former-remove-member-seat ring=board-seat-remove zoom=board-seat-remove
::figure board-former-remove-member-confirm ring=confirm-ok
:::

:::warning
⚠️ Removing deletes the member from this term, and it can't be undone. Their committee seats become free. Their attendance at past meetings stays under their name. You find it on the meeting page, under “Removed from the board”. It also stays in the minutes printed with the “Minutes” button. Tasks given to them become “Unassigned”.
:::

:::tip
For someone who served on the board, choose “Former”, not removal. They stay under “Former members”, with all their records. Removal is only for mistakes.
:::
