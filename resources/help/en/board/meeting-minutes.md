After the meeting, complete its page: attendance, minutes, decisions and tasks. Then print the minutes.

:::example
“اجتماع المكتب العادي” (regular bureau meeting) was held at the club's office. Six members attended, and رشيد مقران sent his apologies. The bureau approved the season's budget.
:::

:::step Open the meeting from the “Meetings” page
On a phone, first open the menu with the ☰ button. In the menu, click “Meetings” under “Board of Directors”. Then click: اجتماع المكتب العادي. The meeting page opens.
::figure board-schedule-meeting-list ring=nav-board-meetings,meet-row-2
:::

:::step In “Status”, choose “Held”
“Status” has only two choices: “Scheduled” and “Held”. Did the date or place change? Fix “Date” or “Location”. These fields are saved later with the “Save Minutes” button. Without the “Edit” permission on “Board”, the fields show but don't change.
::figure board-meeting-minutes-page ring=meet-status
:::

:::step Record “Attendance”, then click “Save Attendance”
In the “Attendance” card, each member has three buttons: “P” Present, “A” Absent and “E” Excused. A member with no saved attendance starts with no mark. Click “P” for each member present, and “E” for رشيد مقران. The number at the top of the card counts the “P” marks, plus those present under “Removed from the board”. Nothing is saved before “Save Attendance”. That button stays greyed out until at least one member has a mark. Click “Save Attendance”. The message “Attendance saved.” appears. Saving needs the “Edit” permission on “Board”.
::figure board-meeting-minutes-page ring=meet-att-12-excused,meet-att-save zoom=meet-att-12-excused
:::

:::step Write the minutes and decisions, then click “Save Minutes”
In “Minutes”, write what was said in the meeting. In “Decisions”, write one decision per field: المصادقة على ميزانية الموسم. Click “Add item” for a new decision: تكليف أمين المال بملف منحة البلدية. Then click “Save Minutes”. This button saves “Title”, “Type”, “Status”, “Date” and “Location”. It also saves “Agenda”, “Minutes” and “Decisions”. It doesn't save attendance or tasks. The message “Meeting updated.” appears. It needs the “Edit” permission on “Board”.
::figure board-meeting-minutes-minutes ring=meet-minutes,meet-decisions,meet-decisions-add,meet-save-minutes
:::

:::step Attach the signed “Minutes File”
Under “Minutes File”, click the choose-file button and pick the signed minutes. Then click the upward arrow button. Hover over it to see its name: “Upload”. The app accepts a PDF, a Word file or an image, up to 10 MB. The message “Minutes file uploaded.” appears. A line shows your file's name as it was on your device. Click it to open it. A new file replaces the old one. The file is private: only signed-in users with the “View” permission on “Board” can open it. Uploading needs the “Edit” permission. To delete the file, use the trash button on its line, named “Remove”. It needs only the “Delete” permission.
::figure board-meeting-minutes-file ring=meet-file-input,meet-file-upload zoom=meet-file-upload
:::

:::step Give a member a task in “Action Items”
In “Task title”, type: حجز قاعة الجمعية العامة. In the list next to it, choose: بن صالح فاطمة الزهراء. Then choose a due date, if you want one. Click the button with the + sign. Hover over it to see its name: “Add Task”. The message “Task created.” appears. The task shows under “Action Items” and on the “Tasks” page. The member list shows the active members of the current term, plus anyone already given a task in this meeting. Adding a task needs only the “Add” permission on “Board”. To delete it, use the trash button on its line. Hover over it to see its name: “Delete”. It needs only the “Delete” permission.
::figure board-meeting-minutes-task ring=meet-task-title,meet-task-member,meet-task-due,meet-task-add
:::

:::step Print the “Minutes”
At the top of the page, click the printer button. Hover over it to see its name: “Minutes”. A PDF file opens in a new window. It has the agenda, attendance, minutes, decisions and action items. It prints only what is saved, so save before you print.
::figure board-meeting-minutes-page ring=meet-print zoom=meet-print
:::

:::tip
The “Attendance” list shows the active members of the current term, plus anyone whose attendance was already recorded. A member removed from the board keeps their past attendance. You find it under “Removed from the board”, read-only.
:::
