Every board member has a role, such as “President” or “Treasurer”. They may also sit on a committee. You add the roles and committees once, then pick them on the “Board Members” page.

:::example
The club adds the role “مستشار” (adviser) at the “Bureau” level. Then it adds the committee “لجنة الشباب” (youth committee).
:::

:::step Open the “Board Roles” page
On a phone, first open the menu with the ☰ button. In the menu, click “Board Roles” under “App Configuration”. Can't see it? Click “App Configuration” to show it. The table shows each role. The “Board Members” column counts the active members holding it in the current term (former members don't count).
::figure board-roles-committees-roles ring=nav-board-roles
:::

:::step Type the “Name” and choose the “Organigram row” and “Order”
In “Name”, type: مستشار. In “Organigram row”, choose: Bureau. The row sets where the role sits in the organigram. “Top (president)” is on the first row, “Bureau” on the second. The “Member” level has no box: the holder shows in their committee, or under “Other members”. In “Order”, type: 5. The smaller number shows first. Can't see the fields? You need the “Add” permission on “Board Roles”.
::figure board-roles-committees-role-typed ring=brole-name,brole-level,brole-order
:::

:::step Click “Add”
The message “Role created successfully.” appears. The role shows in the table. In the organigram, an empty box for it appears on the bureau row.
::figure board-roles-committees-role-typed ring=brole-add zoom=brole-add
:::

:::step Fix a role's name with the pencil button
For example, you want “Secretary” to become “General Secretary”. On its row, click the pencil button. Hover over it to see its name: “Edit”. A field opens with the name as you read it. Fix it, then click “Save” on the same row. The message “Role updated successfully.” appears. Members keep their role, under the new name. The button needs the “Edit” permission on “Board Roles”.
::figure board-roles-committees-role-rows ring=brole-edit-secretary zoom=brole-edit-secretary
::figure board-roles-committees-role-edit ring=brole-edit-name,brole-edit-save
:::

:::step Open the “Committees” page
On a phone, first open the menu with the ☰ button. In the menu, click “Committees” under “App Configuration”. You find it below “Board Roles”. You need the “View” permission on “Committees”. This list has its own permissions.
::figure board-roles-committees-committees ring=nav-board-committees
:::

:::step Type the committee name and the “Order”
In “Name”, type: لجنة الشباب. In “Order”, type: 4. The smaller number shows first in the organigram. The app refuses the name of a committee that already exists. Can't see the fields? You need the “Add” permission on “Committees”.
::figure board-roles-committees-committee-typed ring=bcom-name,bcom-order
:::

:::step Click “Add”
The message “Committee added.” appears. The committee shows in the table. In the organigram, its empty boxes appear: “Head”, two “Committee deputy” boxes, and “Add member”.
::figure board-roles-committees-committee-typed ring=bcom-add zoom=bcom-add
:::

:::tip
A role or committee used in any term, even an old one, can't be deleted. The number in the table may be 0, because it only counts active members of the current term. Its trash button then does nothing. Hover over it to see “Role is assigned to members” or “Committee has members”. You need the “Delete” permission to see the button.
:::
