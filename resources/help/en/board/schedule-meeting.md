Every new meeting starts “Scheduled”. You add it from the “Meetings” page or from the “Calendar”. After it is held, you record its attendance, minutes and decisions.

:::example
The bureau meets next Tuesday at 18:00, at the club's office. Meeting title: اجتماع تحضير الموسم (season preparation).
:::

:::step Open the “Meetings” or “Calendar” page
On a phone, first open the menu with the ☰ button. In the menu, click “Meetings” under “Board of Directors”. The meetings appear, the latest date at the top. The “All”, “Scheduled”, “Held” and “Cancelled” buttons filter the list. Prefer the calendar? Click “Calendar” instead, and go to step 5.
::figure board-schedule-meeting-list ring=nav-board-meetings
::figure board-schedule-meeting-calendar ring=nav-board-calendar
:::

:::step In “Meetings”, click the “Add Meeting” button
Above the list, click the button with the + sign. Hover over it to see its name: “Add Meeting”. The new meeting's card appears above the list. Can't see the button? You need the “Add” permission on “Board”.
::figure board-schedule-meeting-list ring=meet-add zoom=meet-add
:::

:::step Fill in the card and the “Agenda” items
In “Title”, type: اجتماع تحضير الموسم. In “Type”, leave “Ordinary”. The other two choices are “Extraordinary” and “General Assembly”. In “Date”, choose the day and time: next Tuesday, 18:00. In “Location”, type: مقر النادي. “Title” and “Date” are required. In “Agenda”, type the first item: تقييم الموسم الماضي. Click “Add item” below it for a second item: ميزانية الموسم الجديد. The round button next to an item deletes it. An empty field is not saved.
::figure board-schedule-meeting-form ring=meet-form-title,meet-form-type,meet-form-date,meet-form-location
::figure board-schedule-meeting-agenda ring=meet-form-agenda,meet-form-agenda-add zoom=meet-form-agenda-add
:::

:::step Click “Create”
The message “Meeting created.” appears. The new meeting's page opens, with the status “Scheduled”. There you can fix any field later, then click “Save Minutes”. The “Meetings” route ends here.
::figure board-schedule-meeting-agenda ring=meet-form-create zoom=meet-form-create
:::

:::step In “Calendar”, click the + sign on the meeting day
The calendar shows the month, with meetings, tasks and subscription due dates. Each day has a small + sign next to its number, always visible, on phones too. Click the + sign in next Tuesday's box. Hover over it to see “Add Meeting”. The sign needs the “Add” permission on “Board”.
::figure board-schedule-meeting-calendar ring=meet-cal-add zoom=meet-cal-day
:::

:::step Fill in the “Add Meeting” window
“Date” holds the day you chose, at 18:00. In “Title”, type: اجتماع تحضير الموسم. In “Location”, type: مقر النادي. “Type” stays “Ordinary”. This window has no “Agenda”.
::figure board-schedule-meeting-calendar-form ring=meet-cal-form-title,meet-cal-form-date,meet-cal-form-location
:::

:::step Click “Create” in the window
The message “Meeting created.” appears. The new meeting's page opens, with the status “Scheduled”. Add the “Agenda” items there, then click “Save Minutes”.
::figure board-schedule-meeting-calendar-form ring=meet-cal-form-create zoom=meet-cal-form-create
:::
