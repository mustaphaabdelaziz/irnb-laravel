A term is the board's period of office, such as four years. Each term has its own members and organigram. One term is the current term.

:::example
“العهدة 2025–2029” (term 2025–2029) ends soon. The club prepares “العهدة 2029–2033” and starts it with the current term's members.
:::

:::step Open the “Board Members” page
On a phone, first open the menu with the ☰ button. In the menu, click “Board Members” under “Board of Directors”. The page opens on the current term. Its name is in the “Term” list, followed by ★. Next to the list are its start date then its end date, with a dash between them: 2025-03-01 – 2029-02-28.
::figure board-terms-page ring=nav-board-members zoom=board-term-select
:::

:::step Click the “New Term” button
At the top of the page, click the button with the + sign. Hover over it to see its name: “New Term”. A window with the same name opens. Can't see the button? You need the “Add” permission on “Board”.
::figure board-terms-page ring=board-term-new zoom=board-term-new
:::

:::step Type the “Name” and the two dates
In “Name”, type: العهدة 2029–2033. In “Term Start”, choose: 01/03/2029. In “Term End”, choose: 28/02/2033. Both dates are optional. The end can't be before the start. Leave “Set as current term” unticked for now, as the term hasn't started yet.
::figure board-terms-new-form ring=board-term-name,board-term-start,board-term-end
:::

:::step Click “Save”
The message “Term created.” appears. The page stays on the term you were on. The new term shows in the “Term” list.
::figure board-terms-new-form ring=board-term-save zoom=board-term-save
:::

:::step Choose the new term in “Term”
In the “Term” list, choose: العهدة 2029–2033. Its organigram appears, with every box empty.
::figure board-terms-next ring=board-term-select
:::

:::step Click “Copy the previous term’s members” and confirm
Click the button with two overlapping squares. Hover over it to see its name: “Copy the previous term’s members”. The previous term is the one that starts just before, here “العهدة 2025–2029”. A window asks: “7 active members of “العهدة 2025–2029” will be copied with their roles and committee seats.” Then: “Former members are not copied.”, such as مراد زيتوني. Click “Copy the previous term’s members”. The message “Previous term’s members copied.” appears. The button only shows in a term with no members, right after a term that has active members. It needs the “Add” permission on “Board”.
::figure board-terms-next ring=board-copy-previous zoom=board-copy-previous
::figure board-terms-copy-confirm ring=confirm-ok
:::

:::step Make it the current term with the “Edit Term” button
Has the term started? Click the pencil button next to the button with the + sign. Hover over it to see its name: “Edit Term”. Tick “Set as current term”, then click “Save”. The message “Term updated.” appears. The ★ moves to this term, since only one term is current. In the same window you can fix the name and the two dates. The button needs the “Edit” permission on “Board”.
::figure board-terms-next ring=board-term-edit zoom=board-term-edit
::figure board-terms-edit-form ring=board-term-current,board-term-save
:::

:::tip
The current term matters. Its members are the base for “Overview”, the meeting attendance list, and the people assigned in “Tasks”. The new term's members are a separate copy: what you change there doesn't touch the old term.
:::
