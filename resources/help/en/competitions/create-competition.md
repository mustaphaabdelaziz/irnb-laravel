A competition is a league, a cup or a tournament played by one team in one season. It has a squad that starts with the team's whole roster. The competition's matches call players up from this squad.

:::example
We create “كأس الولاية لأقل من 17 سنة” (the wilaya under-17 cup) for the team “الفريق أ” (أقل من 17 سنة). Then we check its squad, with the team's seven players.
:::

:::step Open the “Competitions” page
On a phone, first open the menu with the ☰ button. In the menu, click “Competitions” under “Competitions & matches”. Can't see it? Click “Competitions & matches” to show it. The competition is created in the season shown at the top of the page, such as 2026/27.
::figure comp-create-competition-list ring=nav-competitions
:::

:::step Click “New competition”
A form appears above the table. You need the “Add” right in “Competitions”. A coach limited to their own teams sees the button if they coach a team this season. They can only pick their own teams.
::figure comp-create-competition-list ring=compn-new zoom=compn-new
:::

:::step Fill in the form
In “Team”, choose: الفريق أ · أقل من 17 سنة. In “Name”, type: كأس الولاية لأقل من 17 سنة. “Type” has four choices: “League”, “Cup”, “Tournament” and “Friendly”. Choose: Cup. Leave “Game format” on “Team's format”. The competition is then played in the team's format, here “Football 5”. Choose another format if you want. Only the formats of the team's sport appear.
::figure comp-create-competition-form ring=compn-team,compn-name,compn-type,compn-format
:::

:::step Click “Add”
The button stays greyed out until you choose a team. The message “Competition created.” appears, and the competition page opens on the “Squad” tab. Its squad starts with every player on the team's roster this season.
::figure comp-create-competition-form ring=compn-add zoom=compn-add
:::

:::step Untick the players who will not play
The squad shows the players on the team's roster this season, with their numbers and badges. At first, every player is ticked. Untick the players who will not play in the competition. In our example everyone plays, so we leave the ticks as they are. A player who is not on the roster does not appear here. Add him from the team page first. A player who leaves the roster also leaves the squad. The picture shows an existing competition, with a full squad.
::figure comp-create-competition-squad ring=compn-squad-list
:::

:::step Click “Save”
The message “Squad saved.” appears. Click this button after any change to the ticks; the change is not kept before that. In our example we changed nothing, so there is no need to save. You need the “Edit” right in “Competitions”. A coach limited to their own teams saves squads only for the competitions of the teams they coach.
::figure comp-create-competition-squad ring=compn-squad-save zoom=compn-squad-save
:::

:::step Open the “Settings” tab
Click “Settings”. Under “Suspension rules” there are three boxes. The first is “Matches banned after a red card”. The second is “Yellow cards before a ban”. The third is “Matches banned for yellow cards”. A new competition starts with a one-match ban after a red card, and no ban for yellow cards. The app works out the bans from the match reports. The details are in the suspensions article. In our example we leave them as they are. The picture shows an existing competition, with three yellow cards before a ban.
::figure comp-create-competition-rules ring=compn-tab-rules,compn-rule-red,compn-rule-yellow,compn-rule-yellow-ban
:::
