A team groups the players of one category in a team sport. It is created once and stays for every season. Its players and coaches change from one season to the next.

:::example
We create the team “الفريق أ” (team A) for the category “أقل من 12 سنة” (under 12) in “Football”, with the “Football 5” format.
:::

:::step Open the “Teams” page
On a phone, first open the menu with the ☰ button. In the menu, click “Teams” under “Competitions & matches”. Can't see it? Click “Competitions & matches” to show it. Each team is in a card, with its number of players and its head coach.
::figure comp-create-team-list ring=nav-teams
:::

:::step Click “New team”
At the top of the page, click “New team”. A form appears. You need the “Add” right in “Teams”. A coach limited to their own teams does not see this button.
::figure comp-create-team-list ring=team-new zoom=team-new
:::

:::step Choose “Sport” and “Category”, then type the “Name”
In “Sport”, choose: Football. In “Category”, choose: أقل من 12 سنة. Only the categories of the chosen sport appear. In “Name”, type: الفريق أ. The same name can be used in two categories, but not twice in the same category.
::figure comp-create-team-form ring=team-branch,team-category,team-name
:::

:::step Choose the “Game format”
In “Game format”, choose: Football 5. Only the formats of the chosen sport appear. This is the default format for the team's matches. You add formats from the “Game formats” page under “App Configuration”.
::figure comp-create-team-form ring=team-format
:::

:::step Click “Add”
The message “Team created.” appears, and the team page opens.
::figure comp-create-team-form ring=team-add zoom=team-add
:::

:::step Get to know the team tabs
The team page has six tabs: “Roster”, “Coaches”, “Competitions”, “Matches”, “Stats” and “Team”. “Stats” shows for users with the “View” right in “Matches”. The “Team” tab holds the name, the category, the game format and archiving. The picture shows the page of an existing team.
::figure comp-create-team-page ring=team-tab-roster,team-tab-coaches,team-tab-competitions,team-tab-games,team-tab-stats,team-tab-settings
:::

:::step Change the season with the list at the top
The list at the top of the page shows the season, such as 2026/27. Change it to see another season's roster, coaches and matches.
::figure comp-create-team-page ring=team-season zoom=team-season
:::

:::warning
⚠️ Deleting a team with no competitions and no matches is final. A team that has competitions or matches is archived instead. Archived teams show with the “Show archived” option. The “Delete” button is in the “Team” tab. It needs the “Delete” right in “Teams”. It does not show for a coach limited to their own teams.
:::
