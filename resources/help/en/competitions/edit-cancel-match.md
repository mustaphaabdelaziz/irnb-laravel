A match has four statuses: “Scheduled”, “Played”, “Postponed” and “Cancelled”. You change it from the “Match” tab. From there you also change the date or delete the match.

:::example
The match “الفريق أ — شباب الحي” was put off, so it is “Postponed”. The club now decides to cancel it, so we change its status to “Cancelled”. Then we see what happens when you change the status of a played match, “الفريق أ — اتحاد الشباب” (3–1). Finally we see the confirmation for deleting the match.
:::

:::step Open the match
Open “Matches” as in “How do I schedule a match”. Under “Past”, click the row “الفريق أ — شباب الحي”. It shows the status “Postponed”. The match page opens on the “Match” tab.
::figure comp-edit-cancel-list ring=game-row-5
:::

:::step Choose “Cancelled” in “Status”
The list is below the main fields. Its choices: “Scheduled”, “Played”, “Postponed” and “Cancelled”. Choose “Cancelled”. Nothing changes before you save.
::figure comp-edit-cancel-status ring=game-status
:::

:::step Click “Save”
The message “Match updated.” appears. You need the “Edit” right in “Matches”. A coach limited to their own teams edits only the matches of the teams they coach. The call-up, the line-up and the report stay as they are.
::figure comp-edit-cancel-status ring=game-info-save zoom=game-info-save
:::

:::step Click “Delete”, then “Cancel”
The button is at the bottom of the tab, on the side opposite the “Save” button. It shows only for users with the “Delete” right in “Matches” who manage the team. A “Confirm Action” window opens, with “Are you sure?”. The “Delete” button in the window deletes the match. After that you go back to “Matches” and read “Match deleted.”. In our example we keep the match cancelled, so we click “Cancel”.
::figure comp-edit-cancel-status ring=game-delete zoom=game-delete
::figure comp-edit-cancel-delete ring=confirm-cancel
:::

:::step Open a played match
Open “Matches” as in step 1. Under “Past”, click the row “الفريق أ — اتحاد الشباب”. It shows the score 3 – 1. Do not mix it up with the row of the same name under “Upcoming”. That one is the round 4 match.
::figure comp-edit-cancel-list ring=game-row-1
:::

:::step Choose “Postponed” in “Status”
The two score boxes disappear with this choice. Nothing changes before you save.
::figure comp-edit-cancel-played ring=game-status
:::

:::step Click “Save”, then “Cancel”
The app asks: “Erase the score?”. It says: “The match score (3 – 1) will be erased when you save it as “Postponed”.”. The “Erase score” button in the window confirms the erasing. “Cancel” keeps the score. In our example we click “Cancel”. Erasing touches only the score. The events and ratings stay. The competition's suspensions are worked out again, because the match is no longer “Played”.
::figure comp-edit-cancel-played ring=game-info-save zoom=game-info-save
::figure comp-edit-cancel-erase ring=confirm-cancel
:::

:::warning
⚠️ Deleting is final. It erases the call-up, the line-up, the report events and the player ratings. The competition's suspensions are worked out again. To keep a record, choose the status “Cancelled” instead of deleting.
:::

:::tip
For a new date instead of cancelling, change “Date” and “Kick-off”, then choose the status “Scheduled”. Then click “Save”. The app does not change the status by itself when the date changes.
:::
