The starting line-up is the players who start the match, each in their spot. A called-up player you do not put in a spot stays on the bench. The line-up follows the saved call-up.

:::example
We set the line-up for the match “الفريق أ — اتحاد الشباب”, round 4. The formation is “2-2”. بلعيد عبد الرحمن is back from his ban, so he is the goalkeeper. لعريبي أنس and بوزيد ياسين are in defence. قاسمي أمين, the captain, and بن علي أحمد are in attack. عمراني زكرياء is on the bench.
:::

:::step Open the “Line-up” tab
Open the match as in “How do I call up players for a match”. Click “Line-up”. The call-up comes first. Before it is saved, you read “Call players up first:” next to a “Call-up” link. In our example six players are called up.
::figure comp-lineup-tab ring=game-tab-lineup
:::

:::step Check the “Formation”
The list shows the formations of the game format. “Football 5” has two formations: “1-2-1” and “2-2”. The page starts on the last formation the team used in this format. If it has not used one yet, the page starts on the first one. In our example it starts on “2-2”, so do not change it. A format with no formations has a single layout called “Default”. Before you place anyone, you read “Starters 0/5 · Bench 6/5”. A warning appears: “Only 0 starters out of 5.”. The warning does not block saving. The big-bench warning does not show before you place a player.
::figure comp-lineup-tab ring=lineup-formation
:::

:::step Put the players in their spots
Under each player's name in “Bench (drag onto the pitch)” there is a “Put on spot…” list. Open it, then choose a spot. The “2-2” spots, in order: GK, then DF twice, then ST twice. In our example we put بلعيد عبد الرحمن in GK. We put لعريبي أنس and بوزيد ياسين in DF. We put قاسمي أمين and بن علي أحمد in ST. You can also drag a player's name onto a spot on the pitch. Spot already taken? Its player goes back to the bench. عمراني زكرياء stays on the “Bench”. The counter becomes “Starters 5/5 · Bench 1/5”.
::figure comp-lineup-tab ring=lineup-put-7,lineup-bench
:::

:::step Mark the captain
The table has a “C” column. Tick it for قاسمي أمين. Only one captain: a new tick moves the armband to the new player. “C” shows on his dot on the pitch.
::figure comp-lineup-captain ring=lineup-captain-3
:::

:::step Mark the goalkeeper
In the “GK” column, tick بلعيد عبد الرحمن. Only one goalkeeper. The column shows in sports that have a goalkeeper.
::figure comp-lineup-placed ring=lineup-keeper-7
:::

:::step Click “Save”
The message “Line-up saved.” appears. Nothing is saved before you click. You need the “Edit” right in “Matches”. A coach limited to their own teams sets line-ups only for the teams they coach. Before saving you can change any player's “No.”. If a number is used twice, the app refuses to save and says: “Two players have the same shirt number.”. For a played match, saving works out the players' minutes again.
::figure comp-lineup-placed ring=lineup-save zoom=lineup-save
:::

:::step Click “Line-up sheet (PDF)”
The link is next to “Save”. After saving, a sheet opens with the saved line-up. Print it or send it.
::figure comp-lineup-placed ring=lineup-pdf zoom=lineup-pdf
:::

:::tip
The “Auto-fill” button fills the empty spots. It prefers the player's usual position: a player whose usual position is FW goes to ST. It puts injured players last. Check the result before saving.
:::
