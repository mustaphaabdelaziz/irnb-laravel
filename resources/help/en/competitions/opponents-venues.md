Every match has an opponent and a venue. You record them once here, then pick them from a list when you schedule the match.

:::example
We record the opponent “وفاق الجبل” from “البويرة”, with its logo. Then we record the venue “ملعب الحي الجديد”.
:::

:::step Open the “Opponents” page
On a phone, first open the menu with the ☰ button. In the menu, click “Opponents” under “App Configuration”. Can't see it? Click “App Configuration” to show it. The list of opponents appears, and the “Matches” column counts their matches.
::figure comp-opponents-venues-list ring=nav-opponents
:::

:::step Type the “Name” and “City”, then choose the “Logo”
In “Name”, type: وفاق الجبل. In “City”, type: البويرة. The “Logo” is an image of up to 2 MB, and it is optional. Click the choose-file button, then pick the image. The image shows next to the opponent's name in the list, and on its match pages.
::figure comp-opponents-venues-opponent-form ring=opp-name,opp-city,opp-logo
:::

:::step Click “Add”
“وفاق الجبل” appears in the list, and the message “Opponent added.” appears. You need the “Add” right in “Opponents”. Can't see the form? You do not have this right.
::figure comp-opponents-venues-opponent-form ring=opp-add zoom=opp-add
:::

:::step Click the pencil button to edit an opponent
On the opponent's row, click the pencil button. When you hover over it, its name “Edit” shows. The name and city become fields, a field to choose a new logo appears, and “Save” and “Cancel” appear. You need the “Edit” right.
::figure comp-opponents-venues-list ring=opp-edit-1 zoom=opp-edit-1
:::

:::step Open the “Venues” page
On a phone, first open the menu with the ☰ button. In the menu, click “Venues” under “App Configuration”. This page has its own right: “View” in “Venues”. The list of venues appears, and the “Matches” column counts the matches at each venue.
::figure comp-opponents-venues-venues ring=nav-venues
:::

:::step Type the “Name” and “Address”
In “Name”, type: ملعب الحي الجديد. In “Address”, type: حي النصر. Leave the “Home ground” box empty, because this venue does not belong to the club. The home ground shows first in this list, with a “Home ground” badge. It also shows first in the venue list when you schedule a match. The app never picks a venue automatically.
::figure comp-opponents-venues-venue-form ring=venue-name,venue-address,venue-home
:::

:::step Click “Add”
The venue appears in the list, and the message “Venue added.” appears. You need the “Add” right in “Venues”. To edit, click the pencil button, as for opponents.
::figure comp-opponents-venues-venue-form ring=venue-add zoom=venue-add
:::

:::warning
⚠️ An opponent or a venue that has matches cannot be deleted. For an opponent, the message “This opponent has matches and cannot be deleted.” appears. For a venue, the message “This venue has matches and cannot be deleted.” appears. Anything with no matches is deleted after you confirm.
:::
