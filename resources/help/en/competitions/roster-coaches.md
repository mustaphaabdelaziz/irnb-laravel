Every team has a roster each season: players with their shirt numbers. It also has coaches each season. The roster is where match squads come from.

:::example
The “أقل من 14 سنة” team starts the season by copying last season's roster. Then we add a player to the “أقل من 17 سنة” roster, and we see its coach سمير بوعزة.
:::

:::step Open the “أقل من 14 سنة” team
On the “Teams” page, click the “الفريق أ” card that says “أقل من 14 سنة”. The team page opens on “Roster”. Above the empty table you read: “This season's roster is empty. Last season had 3 players.”.
::figure comp-create-team-list ring=team-card-2
:::

:::step Click “Copy last season”
The button shows only when this season's roster is empty and there were players last season. The button brings back the players with their numbers. It leaves out archived players, those who left the club and those who left the team's sport. The message “Last season's roster copied.” appears. In our example three players appear. بن علي أحمد wears 9, بوزيد ياسين 7 and عمراني زكرياء 5. After copying you can edit the roster.
::figure comp-roster-coaches-copy ring=team-copy-hint,team-copy-roster
:::

:::step Open the “أقل من 17 سنة” team
Go back to “Teams”, and click the “الفريق أ” card that says “أقل من 17 سنة”. The team page opens on “Roster”.
::figure comp-create-team-list ring=team-card-1
:::

:::step Untick “Only the team's category”, then pick a player
All the “أقل من 17 سنة” players are already on the roster, so the list is empty while “Only the team's category” is ticked. Untick “Only the team's category”. Click “Search a player…” under “Add a player”. Players from other categories appear. Choose: بوعلام هشام بن جمال بن مصطفى. He is added at the bottom of the table, and his category shows before saving. This is how a player from another category plays with this team. The “Only players of this sport” box stays ticked.
::figure comp-roster-coaches-add ring=team-only-category,team-pick,team-only-sport
:::

:::step Type the shirt numbers
The table shows each player's number, name and category. حمداني رياض has the “Injured” badge. A player who left the team's sport has the “Left this sport” badge. In the “No.” box, type a number from 0 to 99. For the new player, بوعلام هشام, type 9. The box is optional. A number cannot be used twice in the same team. If it is, the app refuses to save, and under both boxes you see: “Two players have the same shirt number.”.
::figure comp-roster-coaches-roster ring=team-roster-table,team-shirt-10
:::

:::step Click “Save”
The message “Roster saved.” appears. Nothing is saved before you click. A player who leaves the roster also leaves the “Squad” tab of the team's competitions this season. You need the “Edit” right in “Teams”. A coach limited to their own teams edits the roster only for the teams they coach.
::figure comp-roster-coaches-roster ring=team-roster-save zoom=team-roster-save
:::

:::step Open the “Coaches” tab
Click “Coaches” above the table. You see سمير بوعزة as “Head coach”. To change the coaches, click “Add”, choose the user and the role “Head coach” or “Assistant”, then click “Save”. Changing coaches needs the “Edit” right in “Teams”. Without it, a line of explanation shows under the list. For a coach limited to their own teams, it says: “You cannot change the coaches: your account is limited to your own teams.”.
::figure comp-roster-coaches-coaches ring=team-tab-coaches,team-coach-user-0,team-coach-role-0,team-coach-add,team-coach-save
:::
