Every match is between one team and one opponent. You schedule it with its date and venue, then call up its players. When you schedule it, you can also cancel the team's training session that day.

:::example
We schedule a friendly for the team “الفريق أ” (أقل من 17 سنة) against “نجم الساحل”. It is played next Wednesday at 18:00 at “ملعب حي النصر”. Wednesday is a training day for this team.
:::

:::step Open the “Matches” page
On a phone, first open the menu with the ☰ button. In the menu, click “Matches” under “Competitions & matches”. Can't see it? Click “Competitions & matches” to show it. The page shows “Upcoming”, then “Past”.
::figure comp-schedule-match-list ring=nav-games
:::

:::step Click “New match”
A form appears above the two lists. You need the “Add” right in “Matches”. A coach limited to their own teams schedules matches only for the teams they coach.
::figure comp-schedule-match-list ring=game-new zoom=game-new
:::

:::step Choose the team and the opponent
In “Team”, choose: الفريق أ · أقل من 17 سنة. Leave “Competition” on “Friendly (no competition)”. For a competition match, choose the competition here. Only the chosen team's competitions appear. In “Opponent”, choose: نجم الساحل. Leave “Home / away” on “home”. Its three choices: “home”, “away” and “neutral ground”.
::figure comp-schedule-match-who ring=game-team,game-competition,game-opponent,game-side
:::

:::step Set the day, time and venue
In “Date”, choose next Wednesday. In “Kick-off”, type 18:00. In “Venue”, choose: ملعب حي النصر. The “Round” box is for league matches, so we leave it empty. The round name is free text, such as “Round 7”.
::figure comp-schedule-match-when ring=game-date,game-kickoff,game-venue
:::

:::step Set the meeting time and place
In “Meeting time”, type 17:00. In “Meeting place”, type: “مقر النادي” (club house). They appear on the convocation sheet. Both boxes are optional.
::figure comp-schedule-match-meeting ring=game-meeting-time,game-meeting-place
:::

:::step Leave the cancel-training box as it is
The “Cancel the team's training that day” box is ticked from the start. When you save, the app cancels the team's session planned that day. It shows in “Attendance” with the text “Cancelled: Match against نجم الساحل”. This only affects sessions of the team's category and sport. In our example we leave it ticked, because Wednesday is a training day. Untick it if you want to keep the session.
::figure comp-schedule-match-meeting ring=game-cancel-training
:::

:::step Click “Add”
The button stays greyed out until you choose the team and the opponent. If a session was cancelled, the message “Match scheduled and that day's training cancelled.” appears. If not, “Match scheduled.” appears. In our example the first one appears. Then the match page opens. From there you call up the players.
::figure comp-schedule-match-meeting ring=game-add zoom=game-add
:::

::figure comp-schedule-match-list ring=game-view-toggle

:::tip
The “Calendar” button at the top of “Matches” shows the month. Matches appear in it day by day. Choose a team to see its training sessions too. The “List” button takes you back to the two lists.
:::
