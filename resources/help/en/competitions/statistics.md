Statistics are worked out from the reports of “Played” matches. A player makes an appearance when he is called up and plays in a “Played” match. A “Postponed” or “Cancelled” match does not count.

:::example
We read the statistics of “الفريق أ” (أقل من 17 سنة) for the current season. The team played three matches: two wins and a draw, 6 goals scored and 2 conceded. We look for the top scorer: قاسمي أمين, with three goals.
:::

:::step Open “Statistics”
On a phone, first open the menu with the ☰ button. In the menu, click “Statistics” under “Competitions & matches”. Can't see it? Click “Competitions & matches” to show it. You need the “View” right in “Matches”. A coach limited to their own teams sees the statistics of every team.
::figure comp-statistics-page ring=nav-games-stats
:::

:::step Choose the team
At the top of the page there are three lists: the season, “Team” and “Competition”. The season starts on the current season. Click “Team” and choose: الفريق أ · أقل من 17 سنة. “Competition” then shows this team's competitions. Choose one to limit the figures to a single competition. Changing the team or the season empties “Competition”.
::figure comp-statistics-page ring=stats-team,stats-competition
:::

:::step Read the leader boards and the table
The leader boards: “Top scorers”, “Assists”, “Most played”, “Appearances” and “Cards”. Each board shows five names at most. In our example, the first in “Top scorers” is قاسمي أمين. The “Best rating (3+ matches)” board stays empty until a player is rated in three matches. The table columns: “Player”, “Apps”, “Starts”, “Minutes” and “Goals”. Then “Assists”, “Yellow”, “Red”, “Rating” and “MOTM”. The table is sorted by name.
::figure comp-statistics-team ring=stats-leaders,stats-table
:::

:::step Click the “Export” button
It is the down-arrow button. When you hover over it, its name “Export” shows. Choose “Excel (.xlsx)”, “CSV” or “PDF”. The file follows the chosen season, team and competition. Its columns are the table's columns, except “Rating”. It adds a “Sets” column. Ratings stay in the app, because files get passed around.
::figure comp-statistics-export ring=stats-export,stats-export-xlsx,stats-export-csv,stats-export-pdf
:::

:::step Open “Stats” on the team page
Open the page of “الفريق أ” (أقل من 17 سنة) as in “How do I set up a team's roster and coaches”. Click “Stats”. You see the team's record: three matches played, two wins and a draw. Its goals: 6 scored and 2 conceded. Next to it, “Form”: W, then D, then W. Below are the same leader boards, and an “Export” button for this team. The tab does not show without the “View” right in “Matches”.
::figure comp-statistics-team-page ring=team-tab-stats,team-stats-record
:::

:::step Open “Stats” on the competition page
Open the page of “البطولة الولائية لأقل من 17 سنة” (the wilaya under-17 league). Do it as in step 1 of “How do I track suspensions and add a manual suspension”. Click “Stats”. You see the leader boards of this competition only. There is no table and no export.
::figure comp-statistics-competition ring=compn-tab-stats,compn-stats
:::

:::step Open the “Matches” section on a player's page
Open the page of قاسمي أمين as in “How do I find a player”. Scroll down to the “Matches” section. The season's figures are in boxes, named like the table columns. Below are his matches: the date, the opponent, the score, his role and his minutes. Choose another season from the list at the top of the section. If the player is suspended, a “Suspended” warning shows at the top. The section does not show without the “View” right in “Matches”.
::figure comp-statistics-player ring=player-matches,player-matches-season
:::

:::tip
Click a player's name in the leader boards or the table to open his page. There you see the “Matches” section. The names are links for users with the “View” right in “Players”. Without it, they stay plain text.
:::
