The “Inventory Report” brings the state of the equipment together on one page. You see how much there is, its condition and value, and the overdue loans.

:::example
The general assembly is in a few days. The president asks: what equipment does the club own, and what is it worth? We read the report, then print it for the assembly.
:::

:::step Open the “Equipments” page
On a phone, first open the menu with the ☰ button. In the menu, click “Equipments” under “Equipment”. Can't see it? Click “Equipment” to show it.
::figure eq-inventory-report-list ring=nav-equipment
:::

:::step Click the “Inventory Report” button
At the top of the page, click the clipboard button. When you hold the mouse over it, its name “Inventory Report” appears. The report page opens. The “View” permission in “Equipment” is enough.
::figure eq-inventory-report-list ring=eq-inventory-report zoom=eq-inventory-report
:::

:::step Read the six cards
“Total” is every unit: 120, lost ones included. “Available” is what is in stock and ready to lend: 112. “Rented” is what is lent or assigned right now: 4. Despite its name, it does not mean money. Then come “Under Repair”, “Lost” and “Damaged”. “Damaged” is units in stock that cannot be lent until they are repaired: 3.
::figure eq-inventory-report-page ring=eq-inv-tiles
:::

:::step Read the “Asset value”
The big number is the value of all the equipment: 147 500,00. This answers the question about value. Below it is one row per branch, such as “Football”, with the number of units and their value. The “All branches” row is for shared equipment that belongs to no branch. The value is quantity × unit price at receipt. No price? The app uses the catalog's price. Lost equipment does not count in the value.
::figure eq-inventory-report-page ring=eq-inv-value
:::

:::step Scroll down to “Overdue” and “Condition”
To scroll down, roll the mouse wheel toward you, or slide two fingers on the touchpad. On a phone, swipe the screen up with your finger. “Overdue” lists every loan past its date: two balls with أحمد بن علي. To get them back, read the article “How do I record returned equipment”. “Condition” gives the number of units in each condition, such as “New” and “Damaged”. Below it, “Categories” shows the total, available and rented in each category.
::figure eq-inventory-report-lower ring=eq-inv-overdue,eq-inv-conditions
:::

:::step Click the “Print” button
Go back to the top of the page. To go back up, roll the mouse wheel away from you, or slide two fingers on the touchpad. On a phone, swipe the screen down with your finger. Click the printer button. When you hold the mouse over it, its name “Print” appears. The browser's print window opens. Choose the printer, or save the report as a PDF file.
::figure eq-inventory-report-page ring=eq-inv-print zoom=eq-inv-print
:::

:::step Open the “Equipment” reports in “Reports”
On a phone, first open the menu with the ☰ button. In the menu, click “Equipment” under “Reports”. Can't see it? Click “Reports” to show it. The page has cards: “Inventory list”, “Equipment out”, “Status & valuation”, “Equipment movements” and “Stock-take losses”. Each card opens its report. At the top of each report is the down-arrow button, named “Export”. It downloads the report as Excel, CSV or PDF. And the “Inventory status” card opens this same page.
::figure eq-inventory-report-centre ring=nav-reports-equipment
:::

:::tip
The “Reports” page needs no permission of its own. But each card shows according to your permissions. The equipment cards need the “View” permission in “Equipment”. “Stock-take losses” and “Stock-takes” need “View” in “Inventory”. Can't see a card? Ask the app administrator for the permission.
:::
