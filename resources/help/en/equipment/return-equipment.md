The “Equipment out” page gathers everything that left stock and has not come back yet. You record returns from there.

:::example
The club lent أحمد بن علي two “كرة قدم مقاس 5” (size 5 football) balls. Their return date passed, so “Overdue” showed. Today he brought them back in good shape.
:::

:::step Open the “Equipment out” page
On a phone, first open the menu with the ☰ button. In the menu, click “Equipment out” under “Equipment”. Can't see it? Click “Equipment” to show it. The page opens on “Rentals”. Assigned equipment is under “Assignments”.
::figure eq-return-out ring=nav-equipment-out
:::

:::step Search for the person who has the equipment
In “Search”, type: بن علي. The app searches the names of the people who have equipment. Only the loans of أحمد بن علي stay in the table. In the “Due Date” column, next to the date, “Overdue” shows. It shows when that date has come and the equipment is not back.
::figure eq-return-search ring=eq-out-search zoom=eq-out-overdue-3
:::

:::step Click the “Return” button
At the end of the row, click the button with a ‹ on it. When you hold the mouse over it, its name “Return” appears. The “Return — كرة قدم مقاس 5” window opens. Can't see the button? You need the “Edit” permission in “Equipment”.
::figure eq-return-search ring=eq-out-return-3 zoom=eq-out-return-3
:::

:::step Check the “Quantity” and the “Return date”
In “Quantity”, the app fills in everything not back yet: 2. The player brought back fewer? Type only the number that came back. The loan stays open for the rest until it is all back. “Quantity” only shows if more than one unit went out. The “Return date” is today: change it if the equipment came back before today. This date is written in the “History” on the unit's page, even for a partial return.
::figure eq-return-form ring=eq-ret-quantity,eq-ret-date
:::

:::step Choose the “Condition” and type “Notes”
“Condition” starts on the equipment's current condition, here “New”. Both balls are in good shape, so leave it. In “Notes”, type for example: رجعت الكرتان سليمتين (both balls came back intact). The unit's condition only changes if its row is a single unit, such as “ساعة توقيت 1” (stopwatch 1). On a row with several units, like the balls, the “Condition” is only written in the history. A ball came back damaged? Return it here, then separate it with the “Reclassify units” button.
::figure eq-return-form ring=eq-ret-condition,eq-ret-notes
:::

:::step Click “Return”
The message “Equipment returned successfully.” appears. All units are back? The row disappears from “Equipment out”. And the two balls are available again on the catalog's page.
::figure eq-return-form ring=eq-ret-save zoom=eq-ret-save
:::

:::tip
The same ‹ button is on the catalog's page. The “Held by” column shows everyone who has units from the row. Next to each name is its own “Return” button. It opens the same window.
:::
