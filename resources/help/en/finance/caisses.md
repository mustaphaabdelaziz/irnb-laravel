A caisse is a place where the club keeps money. Each branch has a safe, and inside it caisses, such as a category's caisse. Money paid by players of a category linked to a caisse goes into it on its own.

:::example
You want a caisse for the “أقل من 17 سنة” (under 17) category inside the football safe. At the end of the season, you deactivate it.
:::

:::step Open the “Caisses” page
On a phone, first open the menu with the ☰ button. In the menu, click “Caisses” under “Finance”. Can't see it? Click “Finance” to show it. The page shows one card per branch.
::figure fin-registers ring=nav-registers
:::

:::step Click the “Add caisse” button
At the top of the page, click the button with the + sign. Hover over it to see its name: “Add caisse”. The “Add caisse” window opens. Can't see the button? You need the “Add” permission on “Finance”.
::figure fin-caisse-add-button ring=reg-add zoom=reg-add
:::

:::step Type the “Name” and choose the safe and the category
In “Name”, type: صندوق أقل من 17 سنة. In “Where does it belong?”, choose “Football safe”. “Linked category (optional)” then appears: choose “أقل من 17 سنة”. A category that already has a caisse shows “(already has a caisse)” and can't be chosen.
::figure fin-caisse-add ring=caisse-name,caisse-place,caisse-category
:::

:::step Click “Save”
The message “Account added.” appears. The new caisse shows inside the football card.
::figure fin-caisse-add ring=caisse-save zoom=caisse-save
:::

:::step Click the caisse's pencil button to edit it
At the end of the season, find the caisse in its branch's card. Click the pencil button next to its balance. Hover over it to see its name: “Edit caisse”. You need the “Edit” permission on “Finance”.
::figure fin-caisse-edit-button ring=reg-edit-caisse-first zoom=reg-edit-caisse-first
:::

:::step Untick “Active”
The picture shows the “أقل من 14 سنة” caisse as an example. If money is still in it, a message asks you to move it first. Deleting is not right here: it is for a caisse never used and with no money.
::figure fin-caisse-deactivate ring=caisse-active
:::

:::step Click “Save”
The caisse now shows “Inactive”. It no longer appears in the caisse choice lists.
::figure fin-caisse-deactivate ring=caisse-save zoom=caisse-save
:::

:::tip
“الحساب البنكي للنادي” (club bank account) is a “Club level” caisse of type “Bank”. The app refuses a second bank account at club level.
:::
