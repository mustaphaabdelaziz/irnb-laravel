The “Finance” page gathers the season's figures in one place. You read income, expenses and balance there, follow the budget, and print a report for the bureau.

:::example
The bureau meeting asks: how does this season compare with the last one? And how much of the “Equipment” budget was spent? We answer both questions, then print the report.
:::

:::step Open the “Finance” page
On a phone, first open the menu with the ☰ button. In the menu, click “Finance”, the last item in the “Finance” group. Can't see it? Click the “Finance” heading to show the group. The page opens on the current season.
::figure fin-dashboard ring=nav-finance
:::

:::step Choose the season with the buttons at the top of the page
The coloured button is the season shown. A closed season has a tick mark. For another season, click its button. The strip below the buttons shows the state: “Open” or “Closed”.
::figure fin-dashboard ring=fin-years
:::

:::step Read the four cards
First “Opening balance”, then “Total Income”, “Total Expense” and “Season net”. Under each figure, a small line says what it counts. The small percentage with an arrow compares the figure with last season. That answers the comparison question. “Season net” counts the chosen season only. “Cash in the safes now”, next to the chart, is what is left in every safe and caisse, all seasons together.
::figure fin-dashboard ring=fin-tiles
:::

:::step Scroll down to “Budget vs Actual”
To scroll down, roll the mouse wheel towards you, or slide two fingers on the touchpad. On a phone, swipe the screen up with your finger. For “Equipment” you see 18 000,00 of 30 000,00. 12 000,00 is left. Can't see the card? No budget was set for this season.
::figure fin-dashboard-budget ring=fin-budget-vs-actual
:::

:::step Check the “All Years” table
Below the card, a table puts every season on its own row. Click a season's row to go to its dashboard.
::figure fin-dashboard-years ring=fin-all-years
:::

:::step Click the print button to export the season report
Go back to the top of the page. To go back up, roll the mouse wheel away from you, or slide two fingers on the touchpad. On a phone, swipe the screen down with your finger. The button is at the top of the page, with a printer on it. Hover over it to see its name: “Export”. The report opens in a new page as a PDF. Print or save it from there. The button doesn't work? You need the “View” permission on “Reports”.
::figure fin-dashboard ring=fin-export zoom=fin-export
:::

:::tip
The report prints the season chosen in the buttons. Choose the season before you click the print button. The report holds income, expenses, donations, net balance, and a breakdown by category.
:::
