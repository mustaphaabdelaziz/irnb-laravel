Each season has its own figures: an opening balance, income, expenses and a planned budget. At the end of the season you close it, and its balance carries forward to the next season.

:::example
Last season is closed, and it carried 120 000,00 forward to the current season. The treasurer sets the “Equipment” budget to 30 000,00, then closes the season at its end.
:::

:::step Open the “Finance” page
On a phone, first open the menu with the ☰ button. In the menu, click “Finance”, the last item in the “Finance” group. Can't see it? Click the “Finance” heading to show the group. The page shows the current season's figures.
::figure fin-dashboard-settings-link ring=nav-finance
:::

:::step Click the “Finance Settings” button
At the top of the page, click the first white button from the right, with the settings icon. Hover over it to see its name: “Finance Settings”. Can't see the button? You need the “Edit” permission on “Finance”.
::figure fin-dashboard-settings-link ring=fin-settings-link zoom=fin-settings-link
:::

:::step Check the season in the “Budget” card
Scroll down to the “Budget” card. To scroll down, roll the mouse wheel towards you, or slide two fingers on the touchpad. On a phone, swipe the screen up with your finger. Make sure the current season is chosen. Change it only to edit another season.
::figure fin-budget ring=budget-season
:::

:::step Type the planned amount for each category
Each category has one field. For “Equipment”, type 30000. Type 0 to remove the category's line from the budget.
::figure fin-budget ring=budget-line-eqp
:::

:::step Click “Save Budget”
The message “Budget saved.” appears. The button is at the top of the card, next to the season's name.
::figure fin-budget ring=budget-save zoom=budget-save
:::

:::step Click the “Close Year” button at the end of the season
Go back to the top of the page, to the “Fiscal Year” table. To go back up, roll the mouse wheel away from you, or slide two fingers on the touchpad. On a phone, swipe the screen down with your finger. Click the small button on the open season's row. Hover over it to see its name: “Close Year”. The second row is last season, closed, with the “Reopen” button.
::figure fin-seasons ring=fy-close-current zoom=fy-close-current
:::

:::step Confirm the closing
A window appears: “Close this year? Transactions will be locked and the balance carried forward to next year.”. Click “Close Year”. The message “Year … closed. Balance carried forward to ….” appears.
::figure fin-close-confirm ring=confirm-ok zoom=confirm-ok
:::

:::warning
Closing locks all of the season's transactions. You can't add, edit or delete any of them. Need a correction? Click “Reopen” on the closed season, then close it again after the fix.
:::

:::tip
A new season starts on its own with the first transaction dated in it. You only need “Add Year” to set its opening balance in advance.
:::
