After you log in, the **Dashboard** opens. From here you reach every page of the app.

:::example
You want to open the players page of **Example Club**. These steps take you there.
:::

:::step Get to know the menu
The menu is on the left of the screen. It holds every section of the app. On a phone, tap the ☰ button at the top to show the menu.
::figure app-dashboard ring=sidebar
:::

:::step Open a section
Click the name of the section, for example “Player Management”. Its pages appear below it, such as “Players”.
::figure app-dashboard ring=nav-members
:::

:::step Open quick search
Press the Ctrl and K keys together. The search box appears in the middle of the screen. On a phone, first open the menu with the ☰ button, then tap “Search…”.
:::

:::step Type the page name
Type “player” in the box, then press the Enter key. The “Players” page opens, because it is the first result.
::figure app-palette ring=palette-input
:::

:::step Ask for help on any page
At the top of the screen, click the button with a question mark. An explanation of the page you are on appears.
::figure app-dashboard ring=help-button zoom=help-button
:::

:::step Change the screen colours
Click the moon button to make the screen dark. To go back to light colours, click the sun button.
::figure app-dashboard ring=theme-toggle zoom=theme-toggle
:::

:::step Change the language
At the bottom of the menu, click “FR” for French or “EN” for English. To go back to Arabic, click “ع”.
::figure app-dashboard ring=language-switch
:::

:::tip
You do not need to change the keyboard language. The Ctrl and K keys work even with an Arabic keyboard.
:::
