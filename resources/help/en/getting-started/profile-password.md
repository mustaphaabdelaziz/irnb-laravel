title: How do I change my password

You need the password you use now. If you forgot it, ask the club manager for a new password.

:::example
In this guide, the user **كريم** of **Example Club** changes his password.
:::

:::step Open the “Profile” page
On a phone, first open the menu with the ☰ button. At the bottom of the menu, click your name. The “Profile” page appears.
::figure app-dashboard ring=sidebar-profile
:::

:::step Scroll down to the “Update Password” section
Turn the mouse wheel towards you, or slide two fingers on the touchpad, to move down the page. On a phone, swipe the screen up with your finger. Stop at the “Update Password” section.
::figure profile ring=password-form
:::

:::step Type the old password, then the new one
Type your current password in the “Current Password” box. Type the new one in the “New Password” box. Type it a second time in the “Confirm Password” box. It must be 8 characters or more.
::figure profile ring=password-current,password-new,password-confirm
:::

:::step Click “Save”
Click the “Save” button right below these boxes. The message “Saved.” appears. Next time, log in with the new password.
::figure profile ring=password-save zoom=password-save
:::

:::warning
Do not use your name, your date of birth or the club's name. Pick a password nobody else knows, and never give it to anyone.
:::
