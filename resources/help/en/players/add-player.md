You need the player's full name, their date of birth and the branch they will play in.

:::example
We register **أحمد بن علي**, born on **14/03/2012**, in the **Football** branch, in the “أقل من 14 سنة” (under 14) category.
:::

:::step Open the “Players” page
On a phone, first open the menu with the ☰ button. In the menu, click “Players” under “Player Management”. Can't see it? Click “Player Management” to show it.
::figure players-index ring=nav-members,nav-players
:::

:::step Click the “New Player” button
At the top of the page, click the button with the + sign. When you hold the mouse over it, its name “New Player” appears. The “New Player” page opens.
::figure players-index ring=add-player zoom=add-player
:::

:::step Type the player's name
Type “بن علي” in the “Lastname” field, and “أحمد” in the “Firstname” field. Type “محمد” in the “Father” field, and “الطاهر” in the “Grandfather” field. Fields with a * next to them are required.
::figure players-create ring=player-lastname,player-firstname,player-father,player-grandfather
:::

:::step Choose the date of birth and the gender
In the “Date of Birth” field, click the calendar icon and choose 14/03/2012. In the “Gender” field, choose “Male”. Further down, the “Status” field turns to “Student” on its own, because the player is under 18.
::figure players-create ring=player-birthdate,player-gender
:::

:::step Choose the branch
Under “Branches”, click the “Add a branch…” field and choose “Football”. A “Football” card appears below it.
::figure players-create ring=player-add-branch
:::

:::step Fill in the branch card
In the card, enter in “Join date” the day the player started, for example 01/09/2026. Then choose “أقل من 14 سنة” in “Category”. You can leave the other fields empty.
::figure players-create-card ring=player-branch-card
:::

:::step Click “Save”
The button is at the bottom of the page. To get there, roll the mouse wheel toward you, or slide two fingers on the touchpad. On a phone, swipe the screen up with your finger. Click “Save”. The player's page opens with the message “Player created successfully.”.
::figure players-create-bottom ring=player-save zoom=player-save
:::

:::warning
Type the correct date of birth. From it, the app knows the player is under 18 and sets them to “Student”. The category does not change on its own: choose it yourself in the branch card.
:::
