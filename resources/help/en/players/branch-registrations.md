A player stays one player, however many branches they have. Each branch has its own card, with its category, join date and membership number.

:::example
**أحمد بن علي** plays **Football**. He now also joins **Swimming**, on 01/11/2026, in the “أصاغر” category.
:::

:::step Open the “Edit Player” page
On the player's page, click the button with a pencil on it, named “Edit Player”.
::figure player-show ring=player-edit zoom=player-edit
:::

:::step Choose the new branch
In the “Role & Position” card, click the “Add a branch…” field and choose “Swimming”. A second card named “Swimming” appears, below the “Football” card.
::figure player-edit-branches ring=player-add-branch
:::

:::step Fill in the “Swimming” card
In “Join date”, choose 01/11/2026. In “Category”, choose “أصاغر”. Do not change anything in the “Football” card.
::figure player-edit-new-branch ring=player-branch-card-2
:::

:::step Click “Save Changes”
The button is at the bottom of the page. To get there, roll the mouse wheel toward you, or slide two fingers on the touchpad. On a phone, swipe the screen up with your finger. Click “Save Changes”. The app gives the player a new membership number in swimming, on its own.
::figure player-edit-bottom ring=player-save zoom=player-save
:::

:::step Check on the player's page
You are back on the player's page. At the bottom of “Basic Information” there are now two boxes: “Football” and “Swimming”. This is the page of ياسين بوزيد, who is also in both branches.
::figure player-show-2 ring=player-registrations
:::

:::step Open “Join dates to complete”
Some old registrations only have a year, with no day. To complete them, click the “Join dates to complete” button on the “Players” page. Can't see it? First click the button with three dots, at the top of the page.
::figure players-index ring=join-dates-link
:::

:::step Type the missing date
Next to each player, choose their join day in the date field. Then click “Save” on the same row. The row disappears from the list.
::figure join-dates ring=join-dates-row
:::

Is the branch's category split into groups? A “Group” field appears in the branch card. Choose the player's group there. To change it later, read “How do I split a category into training groups”.

:::warning
The player left a branch? Do not delete their card. Change its “Membership status” to “Left the team”. Deleting is only for fixing a typing mistake.
:::
