---
title: How do I record a player's documents
summary: Record the documents the player brought in, and the number of copies when a document needs more than one.

The player's documents are in the “Documents” card on their page. Each document not handed in yet shows “Missing” next to it.

:::example
**أحمد بن علي** brought his birth certificate, and two photos out of four.
:::

:::step Scroll down to the “Documents” card
On the player's page, roll the mouse wheel toward you, or slide two fingers on the touchpad. On a phone, swipe the screen up with your finger. Stop at the “Documents” card.
::figure player-documents ring=player-documents
:::

:::step Click the receive button under “Birth certificate”
Click the button with a green tick on it. When you hold the mouse over it, its name “Mark received” appears. A small window opens.
::figure player-documents ring=document-toggle zoom=document-toggle
:::

:::step Click “Save”
The “Received on” field holds today's date. Click “Save”. Next to the document, “Received (paper only)” appears.
::figure player-documents-receive ring=document-save zoom=document-save
:::

:::step Click the receive button under “Photo”
This document is asked for in 4 copies. Click the button with the green tick under it. The same window opens, with a “Copies received” field.
::figure player-documents ring=document-photo zoom=document-photo
:::

:::step Type the number of copies
The “Copies received” field holds the number 4. Delete it and type 2.
::figure player-documents-photo ring=document-copies
:::

:::step Click “Save”
Click “Save”. Next to the document, “2 / 4 copies” and “Incomplete” appear.
::figure player-documents-photo ring=document-save zoom=document-save
:::

When the player brings the two missing copies, click the button with two arrows under “Photo”, named “Renew”. Check that the number is 4, then click “Save”.

:::tip
If you put the player's picture in their file, “Photo” counts as received on its own. Without a picture, record it with the button, as we did here.
:::
