All of a player's details are changed from one page: “Edit Player”.

:::example
In the file of **أحمد بن علي**, the grandfather is written “طاهر”. The correct name is “الطاهر”.
:::

:::step Open the player's page
On the “Players” page, click the player's name. Their page opens. Can't find them? Read “How do I find a player”.
::figure players-filtered ring=player-row-first
:::

:::step Click the “Edit Player” button
The button is on the right of the page. On a phone, you find it under the “Basic Information” card. Click the button with a pencil on it. When you hold the mouse over it, its name “Edit Player” appears. The “Edit Player” page opens.
::figure player-show ring=player-edit zoom=player-edit
:::

:::step Fix the field
Click inside the “Grandfather” field. Delete “طاهر” and type “الطاهر”.
::figure player-edit ring=player-grandfather
:::

:::step Click “Save Changes”
The button is at the bottom of the page. To get there, roll the mouse wheel toward you, or slide two fingers on the touchpad. On a phone, swipe the screen up with your finger. Click “Save Changes”. You are back on the player's page, with the message “Player updated successfully.”.
::figure player-edit-bottom ring=player-save zoom=player-save
:::

:::tip
Changed your mind? Click “Cancel” next to “Save Changes”, and nothing is saved.
:::

:::warning
The new name shows on every page of the app. If the player is a member of the “Board of Directors”, their name changes there too.
:::
