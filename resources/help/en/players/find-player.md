You search on the “Players” page. A few letters of the name are enough.

:::example
We look for **أحمد بن علي**, from the “أقل من 14 سنة” category, to open his page.
:::

:::step Open the “Players” page
On a phone, first open the menu with the ☰ button. In the menu, click “Players” under “Player Management”. Can't see it? Click “Player Management” to show it.
::figure players-index ring=nav-members,nav-players
:::

:::step Type the name in the search field
The field is under the statistics. To get there, roll the mouse wheel toward you, or slide two fingers on the touchpad. On a phone, swipe the screen up with your finger. Type “أحمد” in the “Search for a member” field. After a moment, two players appear: أحمد بن علي, and a player whose grandfather is named أحمد.
::figure players-search ring=players-search
:::

:::step Choose the category
Click the “All Categories” field and choose “أقل من 14 سنة”. Only أحمد بن علي stays in the list.
::figure players-filtered ring=players-category
:::

:::step Open the player's page
Click his name in the list: “بن علي أحمد بن محمد بن الطاهر”. His page opens.
::figure players-filtered ring=player-row-first
:::

:::tip
You can also type the family name, the father's name or the membership number. Write the hamza as in the player's file: “احمد” without a hamza does not find “أحمد”.
:::

:::warning
The app keeps your search and filters, even after you close it. Can't find a player you know? Click the red button next to the filters, named “Clear filters”.
:::
