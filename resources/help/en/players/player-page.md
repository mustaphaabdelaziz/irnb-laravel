A player's page opens when you click their name on the “Players” page.

:::example
We read the page of **أحمد بن علي**, a football player in the “أقل من 14 سنة” category.
:::

:::step Look at the top of the “Basic Information” card
At the top are the player's picture and full name, with the membership number below. If there is no picture, the first letter of the name shows instead. Below that are the date of birth, age, gender and phone.
::figure player-show ring=player-header
:::

:::step Look at the branch box
At the bottom of the card there is a box for each branch the player plays in. It shows “Join date”, “Category” and “Membership status”, and the membership number in that branch.
::figure player-show ring=player-registrations
:::

:::step Switch between the two branches
This step is for a player registered in two branches, like ياسين بوزيد. The names of both branches appear at the top of the card. Click a branch name: its membership number and documents in that branch show.
::figure player-show-2 ring=player-tabs
:::

:::step Get to know the buttons
On the right of the page is the “Outstanding Debt” card. Under the amount, a button with a $ in a circle records a payment. On a phone, you find this card under the “Basic Information” card. The card below it has three buttons: the pencil to edit, the card to print the “Member Card”, and the red bin to delete. Hold the mouse over a button to see its name.
::figure player-show ring=player-actions zoom=player-edit
:::

:::step Scroll down to see the rest of the page
Roll the mouse wheel toward you, or slide two fingers on the touchpad. On a phone, swipe the screen up with your finger. You find the “Documents” card, then “Subscriptions”.
::figure player-documents ring=player-documents
:::

:::tip
Can't see a section a colleague sees? Each section shows only to people with the right permission. Ask the club manager for it.
:::
