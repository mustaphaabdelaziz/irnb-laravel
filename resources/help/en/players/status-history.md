title: How do I track a player leaving and coming back

Each branch has its own status history. Every change of “Membership status” stays in it, with its date. Nothing is lost when the player comes back.

:::example
**أحمد بن علي** left **كرة القدم** on 15/06/2025. He came back on 01/09/2026.
:::

:::step Open the “Edit Player” page
On Ahmed's page, click the button with a pencil, named “Edit Player”.
:::

:::step Change the “Membership status”
In the “كرة القدم” card, choose “Left the club”. Two fields appear: “Date of change” and “Note”.
:::

:::step Type the date and the reason
In “Date of change”, choose 15/06/2025. In “Note”, type the reason if you want. Then click “Save Changes”.
:::

:::step Record the return the same way
On the day he comes back, set the “Membership status” back to the registered status. Choose 01/09/2026 in “Date of change”, then save. He keeps his membership number and his first join date.
:::

:::step Read the “Status history”
On the player page, under each branch, the “Status history” shows the join date, then every change with its date, note and who recorded it.
:::

:::step Add what happened before
To record an old departure, click “Add a past line” in the “Status history”. To correct a line, click the pencil next to it.
:::

:::warning
Changing the status of several players at once from the “Players” list writes no line in the history. Change it from “Edit Player” to keep a trace.
:::
