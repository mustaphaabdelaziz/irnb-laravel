A category is a group of players of the same age within a branch, like “أقل من 17 سنة” (under 17). Each branch has its own categories. Two branches can have a category with the same name, but one branch does not accept a duplicate name.

:::example
The Football branch opens the category “أقل من 19 سنة” (under 19). In the same season, it deactivates the category “أقل من 12 سنة”, because there is no group of young children this season.
:::

:::step Open the “Branches” page
On a phone, first open the menu with the ☰ button. In the menu, click “Branches” under “App Configuration”. You don't see it? Click “App Configuration” to show it. The “Categories” button under “App Configuration” opens the main branch's categories directly. For another branch, go through the “Branches” page.
::figure set-branch-list ring=nav-branches
:::

:::step Click the pencil button of “Football”
In the branch's row, click the pencil button. When you hover over it, its name “Edit” appears. The branch page opens.
::figure set-branch-rows ring=branch-edit zoom=branch-edit
:::

:::step Open the “Categories” tab
Click “Categories”, next to “General”. The branch's categories appear in a table, with the add form above it. You need the “View” right in “Categories”.
::figure set-branch-detail ring=branch-tab-categories zoom=branch-tab-categories
:::

:::step Type the “Name”
In “Name”, type: أقل من 19 سنة. The “Description” box and the name boxes in Arabic, French and English are optional.
::figure set-categories ring=cat-name
:::

:::step Click “Add”
The message “Category created successfully.” appears. The new category appears in the table, which is sorted by name. You need the “Add” right in “Categories”.
::figure set-category-typed ring=cat-add zoom=cat-add
:::

:::step Deactivate the category you don't need
In the category's row, click the button with an ×. When you hover over it, its name “Deactivate” appears. The message “Category deactivated.” appears, and “Inactive” shows next to the name. To bring it back, click the same button, now named “Activate”. You need the “Edit” right in “Categories”.
::figure set-categories ring=cat-toggle-first zoom=cat-toggle-first
:::

:::tip
An inactive category does not appear when you choose a new category. But its players and its reports stay as they are.
:::
