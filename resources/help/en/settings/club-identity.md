The club's name, color and logo appear across the whole app and on printed documents. You change them from the “Settings” page.

:::example
The club types its three names, then chooses the color “Blue”, then uploads its logo.
:::

:::step Open the “Settings” page
On a phone, first open the menu with the ☰ button. In the menu, click “Settings” under “System”. You don't see it? Click “System” to show it. The page opens on “Basic Information”. You don't see the page? You need the “View” right in “Settings”.
::figure set-identity-basic ring=nav-settings
:::

:::step Type the club's names and the abbreviation
Type your club's name in “🇩🇿 اسم النادي (عربي)”, then in French and English in the two boxes next to it. In “Abbreviation”, type the short name, up to 20 characters.
::figure set-identity-basic ring=settings-club-names,settings-short-name
:::

:::step Click “Save”
The message “Configuration updated successfully.” appears. You need the “Edit” right in “Settings”. In the other boxes you can type the “Tagline”, the “Description” and the “Founding Date”.
::figure set-identity-basic ring=settings-save zoom=settings-save
:::

:::step Open “Branding & Images”
Click this name in the list of sections. It is beside the page, and on a phone it is a horizontal strip above it. On a phone, slide the strip sideways until the name shows. The “Club color” card appears, with the logo card under it.
::figure set-identity-branding ring=settings-tab-branding zoom=settings-tab-branding
:::

:::step In “Club color”, click “Blue”
The app's color changes at once. A moment later, the app saves the color by itself for all users, so you don't need “Save”.
::figure set-identity-branding ring=settings-color-blue zoom=settings-color-blue
:::

:::step Choose the “Logo” file
Click the file button under “Logo”, then choose a picture. The app accepts any picture up to 2 MB.
::figure set-identity-logo ring=settings-logo
:::

:::step Click “Save”
This is the “Save” under the logo card. The message “Configuration updated successfully.” appears. The logo appears in the side menu, and the page takes the new color.
::figure set-identity-logo ring=settings-branding-save zoom=settings-branding-save
:::

:::tip
The logo appears in the side menu, on the login page, at the top of printed documents and in the browser tab. For a different picture in the browser tab, upload it in “Favicon”, under “Logo”.
:::
