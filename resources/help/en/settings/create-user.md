Nobody can sign up for an account alone. The club administrator creates each staff member's account from the “Users” page. The account works as soon as it is created, with no approval to wait for.

:::example
مراد زيتوني joins as coach of the “أقل من 17 سنة” category. The administrator creates the account **mourad** for him, with the role “Coach”.
:::

:::step Open the “Users” page
On a phone, first open the menu with the ☰ button. In the menu, click “Users” under “System”. You don't see it? Click “System” to show it. You need the “View” right in “Users”.
::figure set-users ring=nav-users
:::

:::step Click the “New user” button
At the top of the page, click the button with the + sign. When you hover over it, its name “New user” appears. The form page opens. You don't see the button? You need the “Add” right in “Users”.
::figure set-users ring=users-new zoom=users-new
:::

:::step Type the “Username” and “Password”
In “Username”, type: mourad. Type the password in both boxes, “Password” and “Confirm Password”. The password has at least 8 characters. The username has 3 to 100 characters, with no spaces. It accepts letters, digits and the signs . _ - @ +. The app turns it into lowercase. The eye button in the box shows what you typed.
::figure set-user-create-login ring=uc-username,uc-password,uc-password-confirm
:::

:::step Type the “Firstname”, “Lastname” and “Phone”
In “Firstname”, type: مراد. In “Lastname”, type: زيتوني. In “Phone”, type his number. “Email” is optional. “Gender” stays “Male” until you change it.
::figure set-user-create-name ring=uc-firstname,uc-lastname,uc-phone
:::

:::step Choose the “Role”
Click “Role” and choose “Coach”. The role decides what the account sees. You don't see the “Role” box? It only shows for the super admin. You create the account without a role, then the super admin sets its role later.
::figure set-user-create-role ring=uc-role
:::

:::step Click “Create”
The message “User created successfully.” appears. The new account appears at the top of the list. Give the coach the username and the password. He logs in with them as in “How do I log in to the app”.
::figure set-user-create ring=uc-create zoom=uc-create
:::

:::tip
Ask the coach to change the password from “Profile” the first time he logs in.
:::
