“Log in as” shows you the app exactly as the user sees it. It needs the “Edit” right in “Users”. You cannot log in as a super admin, nor as a disabled account. Nor as an account that has rights you don't have, unless you are a super admin. The “Activity” page needs the “View” right in “Users”. Each user sees their own activity under “My activity”, in the menu with their name.

:::example
Coach سمير بوعزة says he cannot find the subscriptions page. You log in as him to see what he sees, then you go back to your account. After that, you review his activity this season.
:::

:::step Open the “Users” page
On a phone, first open the menu with the ☰ button. In the menu, click “Users” under “System”. You don't see it? Click “System” to show it.
::figure set-users ring=nav-users
:::

:::step Log in as سمير
In سمير's row, click the button with an arrow going into a box. When you hover over it, its name “Log in as” appears. The app does not ask for confirmation. The home page appears, with the message “You are now logged in as this user.”. The button does not show in your own row, nor for a super admin, nor for a disabled account.
::figure set-impersonate-button ring=users-impersonate-samir zoom=users-impersonate-samir
:::

:::step Click “Back to my account”
At the top of every page there is an orange strip. It says you are browsing as سمير, and names your original account. Before going back, open “Player Management” in the menu: you don't see “Subscriptions”, because سمير's role does not allow it. Click “Back to my account” in the strip. You return to the “Users” page, with the message “Back to your own account.”.
::figure set-impersonating ring=impersonate-banner,impersonate-leave zoom=impersonate-leave
:::

:::step Open the “Activity” page
On a phone, first open the menu with the ☰ button. In the menu, click “Activity” under “System”. You don't see it? Click “System” to show it. The page opens on “This month”.
::figure set-activity-nav ring=nav-activity
:::

:::step Choose “This season”
Choose “This season” from the “Period” list. Under the list there are four cards: “Total”, “Active weeks”, “Average per active day” and “Busiest day”.
::figure set-activity ring=activity-period
::figure set-activity ring=activity-tiles
:::

:::step Open سمير's page
Under the charts, a table shows each user and their activity by area. Click سمير's name in the table.
::figure set-activity-table ring=activity-row-3
:::

:::step Click a number to see its details
سمير's page has one card per area, with a number for each action. Click the first number. The list of actions appears under it, with their dates. It carries the action's title as its heading.
::figure set-activity-user-count ring=activity-count-first zoom=activity-count-first
:::

:::tip
What you do while logged in as someone else is recorded in that user's name, with “by” and your name next to it. In the picture, one of نادية's actions was entered this way.
:::
