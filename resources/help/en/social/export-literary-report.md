After creating the report, you prepare it on its page: the sections, details, signatories and texts. Then you export it as a PDF file to print.

:::example
We open “التقرير الأدبي للموسم 2026/2027”. We keep all the sections and include the details. We choose “بوخالفة عبد المجيد” and “بن صالح فاطمة الزهراء” as signatories. We write a short word from the president, then save and export.
:::

:::step Open the report from the list
In “Literary report”, click the report's title. You need the “Edit” permission for “Literary report”. At the top of the page are the title and the period, and below them “Report sections”.
::figure soc-create-literary-report-list ring=lit-open-1
:::

:::step Check the “Report sections”
Every ticked section is printed in the report, in a fixed order that does not change. Untick a section you don't want. Under a disabled section is this message: “You cannot view this section, so your export will leave it out”. Each section follows the permissions of whoever exports the report. Its state stays as it is when you save.
::figure soc-export-literary-report-sections ring=lit-sections
:::

:::step Tick “Include details” and type a “Note”
Tick the box that reads: “Include details (totals and activity report texts)”. Then the summary of totals by type is printed, along with each activity's “Activity report” text. Then type your remark in “Note”. Under it is this message: “Printed after the activity tables, e.g. details can be checked on the club's official page”.
::figure soc-export-literary-report-details ring=lit-details,lit-note
:::

:::step Choose “First signatory” and “Second signatory”
Choose two different board members: the same member is not accepted twice. Members of the current term show first in the list. After each member's name comes the name of their term. “— None —” leaves the signatory empty.
::figure soc-export-literary-report-details ring=lit-signer-1,lit-signer-2
:::

:::step Write the texts in “Editorial texts”
Write a short text in “President's word”. Do the same for “Introduction” and “Conclusion and outlook”. All three texts are optional. “Introduction” is already filled in on the demo report.
::figure soc-export-literary-report-texts ring=lit-president-word,lit-introduction,lit-conclusion
:::

:::step Click “Save”
Before you save, the top of the page shows “Save your changes before exporting” instead of the “Export PDF” button. After “Save”, the message “Literary report saved” shows.
::figure soc-export-literary-report-unsaved ring=lit-unsaved
::figure soc-export-literary-report-save ring=lit-save zoom=lit-save
:::

:::step Click “Export PDF”
The picture shows the page header after saving. The button is at the top of the page, and needs the “View” permission. The file is built at the moment of export, so it always holds the latest activities. No copy of it is kept. The file opens in a new tab.
::figure soc-export-literary-report-ready ring=lit-pdf zoom=lit-pdf
:::

:::tip
The file has the sections you chose, in their fixed order. The activity chapters are the types that have activities, in “#” order. The photo appendix has one photo per activity: its “Report photo”, or if none is set, its first photo.
:::
