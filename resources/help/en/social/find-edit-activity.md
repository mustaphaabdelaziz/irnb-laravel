Activities pile up over the season. The filters find the activity quickly. From its page, you correct its details or delete it.

:::example
We look for “بطولة السباحة الولائية للأصاغر” (provincial swimming championship for أصاغر) among the activities. We correct the “Result” from “المرتبة الثالثة” (3rd place) to “المرتبة الثانية” (2nd place). At the end, we see how to delete an activity recorded by mistake. The picture uses the championship page only to show the button and the window: we do not confirm the deletion.
:::

:::step Open the “Activities” page
On a phone, first open the menu with the ☰ button. In the menu, click “Activities” under “Social activities”. The cards and the filters show.
::figure soc-find-edit-activity-list ring=nav-activities
:::

:::step Narrow the list with the filters
Choose “المشاركات الرياضية” in “Type”. Then choose “Swimming” in the “All branches” list. Above the filters are three numbers: “Activities”, “Beneficiaries” and “Participants”. They follow the filters. To search by text, type in the search box: it searches the title, location and partners. In “From” and “To”, choose a period: every activity with one of its days inside it shows.
::figure soc-find-edit-activity-filtered ring=act-filter-type,act-filter-branch,act-stats
:::

:::step Open the card
Click the “بطولة السباحة الولائية للأصاغر” card. The activity page opens.
::figure soc-find-edit-activity-filtered ring=act-card-6
:::

:::step Click the pencil button
At the top of the activity page is a pencil button. Its name, “Edit”, shows when you hover the mouse over it. You need the “Edit” permission for “Activities”. You don't see the button? You don't have this permission.
::figure soc-find-edit-activity-show ring=act-edit zoom=act-edit
:::

:::step Change the “Result”
In “Result”, type: المرتبة الثانية. The other fields are the same as in the “New activity” form.
::figure soc-find-edit-activity-result ring=act-result
:::

:::step Click “Save”
“Save” is at the bottom of the form. You go back to the activity page, and the message “Activity updated” shows. “Cancel” takes you back without saving.
::figure soc-find-edit-activity-save ring=act-save zoom=act-save
:::

:::step Click the bin button
You delete an activity recorded by mistake from its page. At the top of the page is a bin button. Its name, “Delete”, shows when you hover the mouse over it. You need the “Delete” permission for “Activities”. The “Confirm Action” window opens, with “Are you sure?”.
::figure soc-find-edit-activity-show ring=act-delete zoom=act-delete
::figure soc-find-edit-activity-confirm ring=confirm-ok,confirm-cancel
:::

:::warning
Click “Delete” in the window to confirm, or “Cancel” to go back. Deleting the activity also erases its photos and attached files. It cannot be undone. And the activity leaves the literary report.
:::
