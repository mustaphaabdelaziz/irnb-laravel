The activity's photos and files are kept on the activity page. One of the photos is printed in the literary report's photo appendix.

:::example
“حملة تنظيف شاطئ المدينة” (city beach clean-up) has three photos. We write a caption on one photo, and make “فريق المتطوعين” (the volunteer team) the report photo. Then we attach the file “اتفاقية الشراكة.pdf” (partnership agreement).
:::

:::step Open the activity page
In “Activities”, click the activity's card. You need the “View” permission for “Activities”. The next steps, except enlarging a photo, need the “Edit” permission for “Activities”.
::figure soc-record-activity-list ring=act-card-3
:::

:::step Click “Add photos”
In the “Photos” card, click “Add photos”. A window opens to choose files from your device. Choose several photos at once, up to 20 photos. The accepted formats are jpg, png and webp, and each photo can be up to 8 MB. The message “Photos added” shows.
::figure soc-photos-files-photos ring=act-add-photos zoom=act-add-photos
:::

:::step Click the pencil button under the photo
Under each photo is a pencil button. Its name, “Caption”, shows when you hover the mouse over it. The caption line turns into a text field.
::figure soc-photos-files-photos ring=act-caption-3 zoom=act-caption-3
:::

:::step Type the caption, then click the save button
In the field, type: جمع النفايات من الشاطئ. Then click the save button next to the field, named “Save”. The message “Photo updated” shows. An empty field clears the caption.
::figure soc-photos-files-caption ring=act-caption-input,act-caption-save
:::

:::step Tick “Report photo”
Under the photo is the “Report photo” box. The app says of it: one photo per activity is printed in the literary report's photo appendix. If you pick none, the first one is printed. In the example, it is ticked on “فريق المتطوعين”. The ticked photo also has a coloured frame around it. If you tick another photo, the tick and the frame move to it and leave the first one.
::figure soc-photos-files-photos ring=act-report-box-4 zoom=act-report-box-4
:::

:::step Click a photo to enlarge it
Click the photo itself. A window opens and shows it large, with its caption. Click outside the window to close it.
::figure soc-photos-files-photos ring=act-photo-open-5
::figure soc-photos-files-lightbox
:::

:::step Click the upload button to attach a file
In the “Attached files” card, click the upload button. Its name, “Attach a file”, shows when you hover the mouse over it. Choose a pdf, doc or docx file or an image, up to 20 MB. The message “File uploaded” shows. One file is uploaded at a time. The files are private. Only signed-in users with the “View” permission for “Activities” can open them. They are not printed in the report.
::figure soc-photos-files-files ring=act-file-add zoom=act-file-add
:::

:::warning
⚠️ The bin button on a photo or a file deletes it after you confirm. Deleting a photo or a file erases it from the device for good. Deleting the whole activity also erases its photos and files.
:::
