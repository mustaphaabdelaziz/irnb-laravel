Everything the club does outside training is an activity: a campaign, a training course or taking part in a tournament. You record it once, and it goes into the literary report.

:::example
We record “حملة تنظيف شاطئ المدينة” (city beach clean-up), of the type “الحملات التطوعية” (volunteer campaigns). One day, at “شاطئ المدينة”, with “جمعية البيئة المحلية”. Swimmers from the “أصاغر” category took part.
:::

:::step Open the “Activities” page
On a phone, first open the menu with the ☰ button. In the menu, click “Activities” under “Social activities”. The activity cards show, newest first. Above them: the number of activities, “Beneficiaries” and “Participants”.
::figure soc-record-activity-list ring=nav-activities
:::

:::step Click “New activity”
The activity form opens. You need the “Add” permission for “Activities”. You don't see the button? You don't have this permission.
::figure soc-record-activity-list ring=act-new zoom=act-new
:::

:::step Fill in the “Title”, the “Type” and the dates
In “Title”, type: حملة تنظيف شاطئ المدينة. Choose the “Type”, then the “Start date”. “End date” stays empty for a one-day activity, with “Leave empty for a one-day activity” under it. For an activity over several days, type the last day in it. Under “Date as text”, the app says: “Printed instead of the date, e.g. every 17th of the month”. Here it stays empty.
::figure soc-record-activity-main ring=act-title,act-type,act-starts,act-ends,act-date-label
:::

:::step Fill in the location, partners, branch and person in charge
In “Location”, type: شاطئ المدينة. In “Partners”, type: جمعية البيئة المحلية. “Organizer” and “Result” are for sports participations. Under “Result” is an example: “E.g. champion, runner-up”. We leave them empty. Choose the “Branch”: Swimming. Under “Categories concerned”, only this branch's categories show. Tick “أصاغر”. Then choose the “Person in charge” from the list.
::figure soc-record-activity-details ring=act-location,act-partners,act-branch,act-categories,act-responsible
:::

:::step Enter the participants and the cost
In “Beneficiaries”, type: 150. In “Outside volunteers”, type: 12. In “Cost”, type: 8500. Under it is this message: “For information only, not recorded in finance”. Then choose the players in “Participating players”, one by one: each shows as a badge. Do the same for board members in “Participating board members”.
::figure soc-record-activity-people ring=act-beneficiaries,act-external,act-cost,act-players,act-board
:::

:::step Write the “Description” and the “Activity report”
The “Description” is one sentence that introduces the activity. The “Activity report” is a longer text. Under “Activity report” is this message: “This text is printed in the literary report when “Include details” is ticked”.
::figure soc-record-activity-text ring=act-description,act-report,act-report-hint
:::

:::step Click “Save”
The activity page opens, and the message “Activity recorded” shows. From there you add photos and files, as in the article “How do I add an activity's photos and files”. “Cancel” takes you back without saving.
::figure soc-record-activity-text ring=act-save zoom=act-save
::figure soc-record-activity-saved
:::

:::tip
The activity goes into its type's chapter of the literary report if its “Start date” falls inside the report's period. The “End date” does not change the chapter.
:::
