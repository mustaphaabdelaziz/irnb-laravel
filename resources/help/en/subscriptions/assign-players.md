---
title: How do I add players to a subscription
summary: Add a whole category to a subscription in one click, or add a single player.

A player owes nothing for a subscription until you add them to it. Add a whole category at once, or a single player.

:::example
We add the category “أقل من 14 سنة” (under 14) to **اشتراك سنوي**. Then we add the swimmer **نور شعبان**, from the “أشبال” category.
:::

:::step Open the subscription page
On the “Subscriptions” page, look at the “اشتراك سنوي” row. Click its eye button. When you hover over it, its name “Details” appears.
::figure subscriptions-index ring=nav-subscriptions,sub-open zoom=sub-open
:::

:::step Click the “Assign Subscription” button
At the top of the page, click the button with two people on it. When you hover over it, its name “Assign Subscription” appears. A small window opens.
::figure subscription-show ring=sub-assign zoom=sub-assign
:::

:::step Choose the category
In the “Category” box, choose “أقل من 14 سنة”. To add all categories at once, choose “All Categories”.
::figure subscription-assign ring=sub-assign-category
:::

:::step Click “Assign”
The message “1 player assigned.” appears. Only زكرياء عمراني was added, because أحمد and ياسين were already in the subscription. “Total Players” becomes 7 instead of 6.
::figure subscription-assign ring=sub-assign-submit zoom=sub-assign-submit
:::

:::step Click the “Add Player” button
At the top of the page, click the white button with a green +. When you hover over it, its name “Add Player” appears. The “Add Player to Subscription” window opens.
::figure subscription-show ring=sub-add-player zoom=sub-add-player
:::

:::step Choose نور شعبان
Click the name نور شعبان. To find her fast, first type “نور” in the search box. Under the list, “Amount will be: 2 500,00” appears, because the “أشبال” category has a special price.
::figure subscription-add-player ring=sub-picked-player,sub-player-search zoom=sub-amount-will-be
:::

:::step Click “Add to Subscription”
The window closes, and the message “شعبان نور بنت لطفي بن إبراهيم added to subscription.” appears. Her name is now in the subscription's list.
::figure subscription-add-player ring=sub-add-confirm zoom=sub-add-confirm
:::

You can't find the player in the “Add Player” window? The window only shows players of the subscription's branches and categories who are not in it yet. If the subscription is for some categories only, like “رحلة صيفية” (summer trip), tick “Also show players of its branches from other categories”. “Other category” shows next to those players.

::figure subscription-outside ring=sub-outside-category

:::tip
A player is never added to the same subscription twice. If you click “Assign” a second time, those already in the subscription stay as they are.
:::
