The annual subscription is what a player pays for a full season. It has a price for students and a price for workers.

:::example
We create **اشتراك سنوي** (annual subscription) for the season **2026/2027**: 3000 DZD for students and 5000 DZD for workers, in the **Football** and **Swimming** branches. The “أشبال” category is cheaper: 2500 DZD for students and 4000 DZD for workers.
:::

:::step Open the “Subscriptions” page
On a phone, first open the menu with the ☰ button. In the menu, click “Subscriptions” under “Player Management”. You don't see it? Click “Player Management” to show it.
::figure subscriptions-index ring=nav-members,nav-subscriptions
:::

:::step Click the “Add Subscription” button
At the top of the page, click the button with the + sign. When you hover over it, its name “Add Subscription” appears. The “Add Subscription” page opens.
::figure subscriptions-index ring=subscriptions-new zoom=subscriptions-new
:::

:::step Type the name and the two prices
Under “Type”, “Annual subscription” is already chosen. Type “اشتراك سنوي” in the “Name” box. Leave the “Season” as it is: 2026/2027. Type 3000 in “Student Pricing”, and 5000 in “Worker Pricing”.
::figure subscription-create ring=sub-name,sub-season,sub-prices
:::

:::step Choose the two branches
Scroll down a little: turn the mouse wheel toward you, or slide two fingers on the touchpad. On a phone, slide the screen up with your finger. Under “Branches”, tick “Football” and “Swimming”. Choose the branches before the categories: if you change them afterwards, some category ticks may be cleared.
::figure subscription-create-branches ring=sub-branches
:::

:::step Choose the categories
“Categories” is right under “Branches”. Tick each category. A table appears under them, with one row per category.
::figure subscription-create-categories ring=sub-categories
:::

:::step Type the price of “أشبال”
In the “أشبال” row, type 2500 under “Student Pricing” and 4000 under “Worker Pricing”. Leave the other rows empty, so they take the two normal prices.
::figure subscription-create-categories ring=sub-category-price zoom=sub-category-price
:::

:::tip
Under the categories, “Player statuses” are all ticked: every player can be put on the subscription, except those who left the club. Untick a status (for example “معتزل”) to keep those players out.
:::

:::step Click “Save”
The button is at the end of the page. Scroll down to it the same way, then click “Save”. The subscription page appears, with the message “Subscription created successfully.”.
::figure subscription-create-save ring=sub-save zoom=sub-save
:::

After saving, no player owes this subscription yet. Add the players to it from its page.

:::tip
The subscription only covers the ticked categories. With no tick at all, it covers all categories at one price. And with no branch, it covers the whole club.
:::

:::warning
A subscription name cannot repeat in the same season. If the name is already used, the subscription is not saved and a red message shows under “Name”.
:::
