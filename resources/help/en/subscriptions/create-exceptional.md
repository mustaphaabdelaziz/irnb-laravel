An exceptional charge is paid only once, and it has no season. For example a trip, or the club shirt.

:::example
We create **رحلة صيفية** (summer trip) at 1500 DZD, for the “أقل من 14 سنة” category of the **Football** branch.
:::

:::step Open the “Subscriptions” page
On a phone, first open the menu with the ☰ button. In the menu, click “Subscriptions” under “Player Management”. You don't see it? Click “Player Management” to show it.
::figure subscriptions-index ring=nav-members,nav-subscriptions
:::

:::step Click the “Add Subscription” button
At the top of the page, click the button with the + sign. When you hover over it, its name “Add Subscription” appears. The “Add Subscription” page opens.
::figure subscriptions-index ring=subscriptions-new zoom=subscriptions-new
:::

:::step Choose “Exceptional”
Under “Type”, click “Exceptional”. The “Season” box disappears. Under the two prices, this sentence appears: “Exceptional charges are assigned by hand and count in a player's debt until paid.”.
::figure subscription-create-exceptional ring=sub-kind-exceptional zoom=sub-debt-note
:::

:::step Type the name and the amount
Type “رحلة صيفية” in the “Name” box. Type 1500 in “Student Pricing”, and 1500 in “Worker Pricing”. The amount is the same for everyone, so type it in both boxes.
::figure subscription-create-exceptional ring=sub-name,sub-prices
:::

:::step Choose the branch
Scroll down a little: turn the mouse wheel toward you, or slide two fingers on the touchpad. On a phone, slide the screen up with your finger. Under “Branches”, tick “Football”. Only the football categories remain in “Categories”.
::figure subscription-create-exceptional-scope ring=sub-branches
:::

:::step Choose the category
Under “Categories”, tick “أقل من 14 سنة”. A row for the category's price appears under it. Leave it empty, so the amount stays 1500.
::figure subscription-create-exceptional-scope ring=sub-categories
:::

:::step Click “Save”
The button is at the end of the page. Scroll down to it the same way, then click “Save”. The charge's page appears, with the message “Subscription created successfully.”.
::figure subscription-create-exceptional-save ring=sub-save zoom=sub-save
:::

After saving, no player owes this charge yet. Add the players to it from its page, as you do for the annual subscription.

:::tip
For a charge that covers the whole club, like the club shirt, tick nothing under “Branches” or under “Categories”.
:::

:::warning
Once players are added to the charge, you cannot change its type to “Annual subscription”. Choose the type carefully before saving.
:::
