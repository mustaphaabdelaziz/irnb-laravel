Made a mistake in a payment amount? Correct it from the player's page. From the same page you give a discount on a subscription.

:::example
**أمين قاسمي** paid 1500 DZD, but only 1000 was entered. And **إسلام مرابط** gets a 10 % discount on “اشتراك سنوي”, which is 3000 DZD.
:::

:::step Click the pencil button in the payment's row
Open أمين's page from “Players”. On a phone, first open the menu with the ☰ button. Scroll down to “Transaction History”. To scroll down, turn the mouse wheel toward you, or slide two fingers on the touchpad. On a phone, slide the screen up with your finger. On a phone, slide the table to the left with your finger to see the buttons. In the 1 000,00 row, click the pencil button. When you hover over it, its name “Edit” appears. The “Edit Payment” window opens.
::figure fix-history ring=player-history-edit zoom=player-history-edit
:::

:::step Type the correct amount
In the “Amount” box, erase 1000 and type 1500. Leave the other boxes as they are. At the top of the window: “This cancels the original payment and records a new one.”.
::figure fix-edit-modal ring=payment-edit-amount
:::

:::step Click “Save”
The message “Payment updated successfully.” appears. In the “اشتراك سنوي” row, “Amount Paid” is now 1 500,00, and “Remaining” 1 500,00. The new payment keeps the old payment's date.
::figure fix-edit-modal ring=payment-edit-save zoom=payment-edit-save
:::

:::step Click the pencil button in the subscription's row
On a phone, first open the menu with the ☰ button. Open إسلام's page from “Players”. Scroll down to “Subscriptions” the same way. On a phone, slide the table to the left with your finger to see the buttons. In the “اشتراك سنوي” row, click the pencil button. The “Edit subscription” window opens.
::figure fix-sub-card ring=player-sub-edit zoom=player-sub-edit
:::

:::step Choose the discount
In “Discount”, choose “Percentage (%)”. A “%” box appears next to it: type 10. Under them, a line shows “Net owed”: 2 700,00, which is 3000 minus 300.
::figure fix-sub-modal ring=sub-discount zoom=sub-net-owed
:::

:::step Click “Save”
The message “Subscription updated successfully.” appears. In the “اشتراك سنوي” row, under “Discount”, the percentage 10% and the amount 300,00 show. And “Remaining” becomes 2 700,00.
::figure fix-sub-modal ring=sub-edit-save zoom=sub-edit-save
:::

If a receipt was printed for أمين before the correction, it now shows “Cancelled” when you verify it. Print him a new receipt.

A payment from a closed season cannot be corrected or cancelled. A message shows: “Season … is closed. Reopen it to change its transactions.”.

:::tip
A discount as an amount? Choose “Fixed amount”, and type the amount in the “Amount” box. The percentage cannot go above 100. To remove the discount, choose “No discount”.
:::

:::warning
The trash button in the payment's row cancels it. It no longer counts after that, and it does not come back on the player's page. Only a trace remains in the activity log. To correct an amount, use the pencil button.
:::
