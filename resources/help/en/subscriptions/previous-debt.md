Before the app, some players owed money to the club. Record this debt on the player's page, so it counts in “Outstanding Debt”.

:::example
**أيوب شريف** still owes 2000 DZD from the **2023/2024** season. We record this debt, then take its payment.
:::

:::step Click the “Add previous debt” button
On a phone, first open the menu with the ☰ button. Open أيوب's page, and scroll down to the “Subscriptions” card. To scroll down, turn the mouse wheel toward you, or slide two fingers on the touchpad. On a phone, slide the screen up with your finger. At the top of the card, click the white button with the + sign. When you hover over it, its name “Add previous debt” appears. The “Add previous debt” window opens.
::figure debt-button ring=player-add-previous-debt zoom=player-add-previous-debt
:::

:::step Type the debt
Type “دين موسم 2023/2024” (2023/2024 season debt) in “Label”, and 2000 in “Amount Owed”. In “Year”, erase the year shown and type 2024. Type only what he still owes, not what he already paid.
::figure debt-modal ring=debt-label,debt-amount,debt-year
:::

:::step Click “Save”
The message “Previous debt added.” appears. In “Subscriptions”, the row “دين موسم 2023/2024” appears, with “Previous debt” next to it. “Outstanding Debt” becomes 2 000,00.
::figure debt-modal ring=debt-save zoom=debt-save
::figure debt-card ring=player-subscriptions-card
:::

:::step Click the “Add Payment” button
On the day أيوب pays, open his page. On a phone, first open the menu with the ☰ button. At the top of the page, under “Outstanding Debt”, click the button with a $ sign in a circle. When you hover over it, its name “Add Payment” appears. The “Add Payment” window opens.
::figure debt-pay-button ring=player-add-payment zoom=player-add-payment
:::

:::step Tick the debt
Leave “Payment category” on “Subscription”. Under “Subscriptions to pay”, tick “دين موسم 2023/2024 (2024)”. The app writes 2000 in “Amount”. Check the “Cash register receiving the income”.
::figure debt-pay-modal ring=payment-subscriptions
:::

:::step Click “Save”
The button is at the end of the window. You don't see it? Scroll down inside the window the same way. Click “Save”. The message “Payment recorded successfully.” appears. In the debt's row, “Remaining” becomes 0,00 and the status “Paid”.
::figure debt-pay-modal ring=payment-save zoom=payment-save
:::

:::tip
Made a mistake in the debt? Click the pencil button in its row. The “Edit previous debt” window opens, where you correct the label, the amount or the year.
:::

:::tip
A faster way: choose “Debt payment” in “Payment category”, and type the amount he paid. The app pays off the oldest debt first, then the next one. Anything above the total debt is recorded as a donation.
:::

:::tip
Where can I see every debt added by hand? Open “Subscriptions”. In the “Financial state by branch” table, click “No branch”. The “Manual debts” page opens, with each debt and the player's name. Click the name to open the player's page.
:::
